## Old Testament Survey- Ecclesiastes (The Preacher)

## Author:

Solomon

### Ecclesiastes- written during the later part of Solomon’s life after his fall from God.

### Overview-

In the book of Ecclesiastes we can hear the heart of Solomon at the latter part of his life.

You can hear great Sadness, despair, as he sees the failure of natural wisdom to solve human problems- all is Vanity or worthless. Conclusion – without God’s wisdom- and only position + riches your not satisfied but weary and disappointed.

## Outline:

Vanity of Human Pleasures

Earthly Material- its happiness + hindrances

True practical wisdom

Relationship of wisdom to the life of man

The Conclusion – Fear God!
