## Slide 6

Introduction (the foundation of leadership is character)<br>
The most valuable component of leadership is not power, position, influence, notoriety, talent, gifting, academic achievement, or management skills. It is character.<br>
Character is the cradle of credibility for the leader. Without the element of strong, honorable character, leadership and all its potential achievements are in danger of dissolution.<br>
Samson was strong but lacked character and lost his power and vision, Joseph was strong in character than power and saved Egypt.<br>
Every leader is only as safe and secure as his character.

## Slide 7

Continuation (Note)<br>
We do not lack people in leadership positions. We are lacking genuine leadership in our leaders.<br>
Many leadership training seminars and academic programs focus on vision, skills, management, teamwork, and many other helpful subjects.<br>
Yet few of these courses address the topic of the leader’s character.<br>
The subject is either downplayed or never covered at all. This oversight is tragic.

## Slide 8

Definitions of character (samson/joseph)<br>
Character is a commitment to a set of values without compromise.<br>
Character is dedication to a set of standards without wavering<br>
Character is self imposed discipline in keeping with moral convictions.<br>
Character is a constant effort to integrate your words, deeds and actions.<br>
Character is the willingness to sacrifice your pleasure for the sake of principles.<br>
Character is the most powerful force a leader can cultivate because it protects leadership. It will enable you to be a success, personally and professionally, as you carry out your purpose, vision, and goals in life.
