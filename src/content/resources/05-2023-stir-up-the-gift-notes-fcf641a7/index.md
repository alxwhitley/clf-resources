## Slide 1

Three Categories of Gifts:<br>
Spiritual Gifts
  <span class="scripture-reference">I Corinthians 12:8-10</span>
  Personal (individual) gifts that every Believer can possess.<br>
  Major operation in context of ministry/service to others.<br>
Gifts of Grace
  <span class="scripture-reference">I Corinthians 12:27-31</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 12:3-9</span>
  Personal (individual) gifts that many believers are skilled, graced, and/or anointed to have and release in service to others.<br>
Five-Fold Ministry Gifts
  <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:7-16</span>
  God-given offices of church/kingdom leadership.<br>
  These gifts are actual persons set in place to bring the saints into maturity in local churches and apostolic spheres.

**Speaker notes:**

Review the major NT passages that reference gifts, graces, and callings/offices. Briefly identify distinctions and clarify focus for this series on the 9 spiritual gifts given in Paul’s Corinthian epistle.

## Slide 2

Spiritual Understanding:<br>
The HS is an intelligent, willful member of the Godhead, whom we must work with and follow just as the disciples worked with and followed Jesus (<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 16:20</span>).<br>
The HS desires to work in and through us (<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 1:19, 3</span>:7; <span class="scripture-reference">Col. 1:29</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 13:21</span>); the Fruit of the Spirit & the Gifts of the Spirit are two manifestations of this truth:<br>
  Fruit of the Spirit – personal impact, character development, inward purifying of motivation and heart attitudes (<span class="scripture-reference">Gal. 5:22-25</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:9-10</span>)<br>
  Gifts of the Spirit – corporate impact, service/ministry development, outward manifestation of God’s heart and attitude (<span class="scripture-reference">1 Corinthians 12:7</span>; 14:12).

**Speaker notes:**

Emphasis on being led by and used by the Spirit; we must yield our members as instruments unto God (<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 6:13</span>; 12:1). The Holy Spirit has an objective in bringing the body of Christ to maturity and seeks to use members of the body to edify other members of the body (<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:13-16</span>)
