Building Together: The Word of God

Why is the Word of God important?

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 112:1-3</span> - “Praise the Lord! Blessed is the man who fears the Lord, who greatly delights in his commandments! His offspring will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed. Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever.”

It is truth that endures forever.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:160</span> - “The sum of your word is truth, and every one of your righteous rules endures forever.”

It is our guide.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:105</span> - “Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”

It strengthens us.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:28</span> - “My soul melts away for sorrow; strengthen me according to your word.”

It keeps us pure.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:9</span> - “How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to your word.”

It cleanses us.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:25,26</span> - “Husbands love your wives, as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her, that he might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word.”

It is able to save our souls.

<span class="scripture-reference">James 1:21</span> - “Therefore put away filthiness and rampant wickedness and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.”

It keeps us from sin.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:11</span> - “I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you.”

Written Word vs. Spoken Word

Written Word - Lógos

“The entire communication process”

The entire Bible - the Word (or communication process) of God

Something said, reasoning or motive, Divine expression

Expressing the thoughts of the Father through the Spirit

Steps of Lógos

Collection - collecting words, thoughts

Counting - calculation, consideration, evaluation, reflection, reason (list or catalogue)

Presenting - proverb, command, legend, promise, written account, even thing

Unity of the Word of God - <span class="scripture-reference">John 5:39, 46, 47</span>

## Spoken Word - Rhema

A part of Lógos (which is the entire communication process)

An utterance

Begins when words are spoken

Examples of Rhema used in scripture

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 6:17</span> - “And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.”

Vine’s Expository Dictionary: The significance of rhema (as distinct from logos) is exemplified in the command to take “the sword of the Spirit, which is the rhema of God”; here the reference is not to the whole Bible, but to the individual scripture which the Holy Spirit brings to our remembrance for use in time of need, a prerequisite being the regular storing of the mind with Scripture.

God speaks his rhema to us in different ways

Still, small voice - <span class="scripture-reference">1 Kings 19:11-13</span>

Prophecy - <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 11:27-30</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 21:10,11</span>

Prophecy will NEVER go against the Word of God
