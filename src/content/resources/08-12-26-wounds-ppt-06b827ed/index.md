## Slide 6

FORGIVENESS<br>
What Forgiveness Is — and Isn't<br>
Forgiveness<br>
Forgiveness ≠ Reconciliation<br>
Forgiveness is a nature of Christ — it releases Heaven into the earth.<br>
Reconciliation takes two people. Scripture speaks of three types of people: the Wise, Fools, and the Evil.<br>
It takes only one person: releasing your right to personal vengeance, choosing not to hold another's sin against them.<br>
You can choose to forgive any person.<br>
But you can only reconcile with the wise.<br>
Entrust justice to God — “Vengeance is mine, I will repay, says the Lord.” (<span class="scripture-reference">Rom 12:19</span>)<br>
A decision that may need to be renewed as painful memories resurface.<br>
