## Doctrine Vs Theology

Doctrine – Teaching or Instruction

Biblical Doctrine – All that the bible teaches or instructs on a particular subject<br>
Theology – The study of God, religious doctrine or matters of divinity. One’s theology can be a collection of doctrine.

What is the basic purpose of all doctrinal study?

“Many have undertaken to compile a narrative about the events that have been fulfilled among us…so that you may know the certainty of the things about which you have been instructed.” <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 1:1,4</span> (CSB)

To equip the believer to do good works. II Timothy 3: 16, 17

16 All Scripture is inspired by God (God-breathed) and is

profitable (useful/beneficial) for teaching,

for rebuking (describes the inward & outward experience of acknowledging our sin/leads to repentance),

for correcting (“to set up straight again”),

for training in righteousness,

17 so that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work.

To prepare the believer to war an effective warfare against Satan. Luke 4, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 6:17</span>

What are the requirements for doctrine?

Doctrine must be sound (free from mixture of option or error). <span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 1:10</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">II Timothy 4:3, 4</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Titus 2:1</span>

3 For the time will come when people will not tolerate sound doctrine, but according to their own desires, will multiply teachers for themselves because they have an itch to hear what they want to hear. 4 They will turn away from hearing the truth and will turn aside to myths.

But you are to proclaim things consistent with sound teaching.

Doctrine must be pure. <span class="scripture-reference">Titus 2:7-8</span>
