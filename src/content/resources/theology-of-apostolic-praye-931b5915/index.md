## Theology of Apostolic Prayer

Prayer is not overcoming Gods reluctance; it is laying hold of His highest willingness. Richard Trench

### I.Apostolic prayer is rooted in apostolic revelation.

### A.Paul describes the essence of apostolic revelation as a mystery being worked out through Jesus.

Mystery in the NT is not an undiscovered secret, but a secret, undiscoverable by human reason, but now revealed by God’s Spirit. Mystery - That which can only be made known to the initiated. Young’s Concordance

### Pauls life was spent seeing Gods mystery revealed and spread throughout the earth.

### Three elements of Gods mystery being worked out through Jesus:

### 1.Gentiles and Jews have been joined together in one new group, the body of Christ.

2.Through Christ and His body (the church), God will fill the earth with His glory/presence/manifold wisdom.

3.This is the continuation of the promise made to Abraham, that in your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 12:3, 18</span>:18, 26:4, <span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 3:8-9, 16, 18, 28-29</span>), and the mandate given at creation, be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 1:26-28</span>).

### Paul prayed for and revealed it:

<span class="scripture-reference">Eph 1:9-23</span> He made known to us the mystery of His will, according to His kind intention which He purposed in Him...18 I pray that the eyes of your heart may be enlightened, so that you will know what is the hope of His calling, what are the riches of the glory of His inheritance in the saints, 19 and what is the surpassing greatness of His power toward us who believe. These are in accordance with the working of the strength of His might 20 which He brought about in Christ, when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places, 21 far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the one to come. 22 And He put all things in subjection under His feet, and gave Him as head over all things to the church, 23 which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all. (22-23 MSG He is in charge of it all, has the final word on everything. At the center of all this, Christ rules the church. (23) The church, you see, is not peripheral to the world; the world is peripheral to the church. The church is Christs body, in which he speaks and acts, by which he fills everything with his presence.)

The summation of the mystery: God is using the church (both Jew and Gentile in Christ) to continue to fulfill Gods original mandate of filling the earth with His glory through His people who bear His image.

Local and universal church: The Greek word for church is used 114 times in the New Testament 96 of which refer to a specific local group of Christians joined together in God's mission under God given leadership (local church). Most of the New Testament letters were written to local churches and from that context are they properly understood. The local church is the primary signpost and agent in the expansion of His Kingdom. The local church is the key player in the outworking of God's mystery (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 16:18</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 1:22-23</span>)

### B.Jesus revealed apostolic prayer.

<span class="scripture-reference">Heb 3:1</span>...consider Jesus, the Apostle and High Priest of our confession.

### Jesus prayed an apostolic prayer (tied to the outworking of the mystery) before going to the cross.

Joh 17:1-26 Jesus spoke these things; and lifting up His eyes to heaven, He said, Father, the hour has come; glorify Your Son, that the Son may glorify You... 5 Now, Father, glorify Me together with Yourself, with the glory which I had with You before the world was... 9 I ask on their behalf; I do not ask on behalf of the world, but of those whom You have given Me; for they are Yours; 10 and all things that are Mine are Yours, and Yours are Mine; and I have been glorified in them. 11 I am no longer in the world; and yet they themselves are in the world, and I come to You. Holy Father, keep them in Your name, the name which You have given Me, that they may be one even as We are...16 They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world. 17 Sanctify them in the truth; Your word is truth. 18 As You sent Me into the world, I also have sent them into the world. 19 For their sakes I sanctify Myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth. 20 I do not ask on behalf of these alone, but for those also who believe in Me through their word; 21 that they may all be one; even as You, Father, are in Me and I in You, that they also may be in Us, so that the world may believe that You sent Me. 22 The glory which You have given Me I have given to them, that they may be one, just as We are one; 23 I in them and You in Me, that they may be perfected in unity, so that the world may know that You sent Me, and loved them, even as You have loved Me.

Three elements of apostolic design in Jesus prayer (this coincides with the elements of the mystery seen above):

### 1.That Gods glory existent within the Godhead would be revealed through His people.

Glory - splendour, brightness, essence of God, self-manifestation of God.

2.The unity of the Godhead among them is essential in revealing Gods glory. Three times (11, 21, 22) He prayed that the same unity among the Godhead would be among them. As they walk together with God and each other the world may know that the Father sent Jesus.

3.In that unity, Gods people are sent into the earth to fill it with His glory (18). God's family together on God's mission.

### C.God filling the earth with His glory through His people is the key goal of apostolic prayer.

### God filling the earth with His glory has always been at the core of His purpose.

This was the creation mandate given to Adam and Eve (as they walked with God bearing His image they were to be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth <span class="scripture-reference">Gen 1:22, 28</span>).

<span class="scripture-reference">Habakkuk 2:14</span> “For the earth will be filled With the knowledge of the glory of the LORD, As the waters cover the sea. (<span class="scripture-reference">Numbers 14:21</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 11:9</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Psalms 72:19</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 6:3, 11</span>:9, 40:5, <span class="scripture-reference">Zech 14:9</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Mal 1:11</span>).

### This mandate is now being carried out through Jesus and His body.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 1:22-23</span> And He put all things in subjection under His feet, and gave Him as head over all things to the church, which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all.

(MSG...At the center of all this, Christ rules the church. The church, you see, is not peripheral to the world; the world is peripheral to the church. The church is Christ’s body, in which he speaks and acts, by which he fills everything with his presence.)

This was in accordance with the eternal purpose which He carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord...20-21 Now to Him who is able to do far more abundantly beyond all that we ask or think, according to the power that works within us, to Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations forever and ever. Amen.

### D.Salvation should be viewed in the context of His purpose.

### It is important to keep central the two aspects of Gods design in salvation.

### 1.Seeking and saving lost people

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 19:10</span> “For the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost.”

<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 4:19</span>... “Follow Me, and I will make you fishers of men.”

### 2.Filling the earth with His Glory

This is more than people going to heaven when they die. It is demonstrating and revealing the power and nature of God and His Kingdom on earth. (<span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 9:7</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Daniel 7:13-14</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:20</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 14:17</span>, Matthew 13 etc.) This church is the primary signpost and agent in the expansion of His Kingdom. (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 16:18</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 1:22-23</span>)

### E.The summation of the key elements of apostolic prayer.

The key elements from both the mystery and the apostolic prayer of Jesus is the blueprint for all apostolic prayer.

### 1.Gentiles and Jews joined together in one new group, the body of Christ.

2.Through Christ and His body (the church), God will fill the earth with His glory/presence/manifold wisdom. Glory - splendour, brightness, essence of God, self-manifestation of God.

3.This is the continuation of the creation mandate "be fruitful, multiply, and fill," (<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 1:26-28</span>) and the promise made to Abraham, that in your seed all the nations of the earth will be blessed. (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 12:3, 18</span>:18, 26:4, <span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 3:8-9, 16, 18, 28-29</span>).

4.It will take the glory of the unity in the Godhead resident within His people to carry out His design. (Jn 17:11,21,22)

5.In that oneness with the Godhead and each other Gods people are sent into the earth to fill it with His glory. (<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 1:22,28</span>, Jn 17:18, <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 1:22-23</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 3:9-21</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Num 14:21</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 11:9</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Psalms 72:19</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 6:3, 11</span>:9, 40:5, <span class="scripture-reference">Zech 14:9</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Mal 1:11</span>)

### Important: All apostolic prayers will tie in to one or more of these elements!
