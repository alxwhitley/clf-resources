## Slide 7

GENERATIONAL INFLUENCES<br>
The cumulative effect on a person of the things that their ancestors did, believed, or practiced in the past; also the residual consequence of an ancestor’s actions, beliefs, and sins being passed down.

**Speaker notes:**

The things we inherit from our ancestors<br>
What we were raised in will influence us, whether we want it to or not<br>
Example: Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob all were involved with deception at certain moments in their lives and in connection with their wives.

## Slide 9

SPIRITUAL INHERITANCE - Truths<br>
Generational sins (or righteousness) provoke God’s spiritual laws:<br>
  Law of Blessings and Curses<br>
  Kingdom Law of Sowing and Reaping<br>
  Kingdom Law of Multiplication<br>
  Kingdom Law of Binding and Loosing

**Speaker notes:**

Work for both Blessings and Curses<br>
Unrepentant sin becomes a generational curse – transferrable to the next generation as an entanglement or snare.<br>
Laws in the Kingdom of God that come into play here<br>
Sowing and Reaping<br>
“For whatsoever a man soweth that shall he reap, also” <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 6:7</span><br>
When ancestors faces troubles times<br>
If held on to God<br>
Sought His help<br>
Repented of their sins<br>
Forgave the hurts and wounds caused them<br>
They sowed GOOD seed<br>
Descendants would reap a legacy of faith – love, joy, blessings.<br>
BUT….<br>
If they turned away from God in unforgiveness<br>
Responded in anger, resentment, revenge, disobedience<br>
Acted out of the pain<br>
Fell back into OT law of an eye for an eye<br>
Result is an open door<br>
When the enemy detects a nature that looks like his,<br>
He comes and joins himself to it.<br>
These every spirits begin to plant seeds of lust, hatred, rejection<br>
Once our ancestors agree and participate in those behaviors<br>
Their descendant reap all sorts of dysfunction, curses and demonic influences.<br>
Takes us to the next Law of the Kingdom at play..<br>
Law of Multiplication (<span class="scripture-reference">Matt 13:8</span>) What we sow will multiply!<br>
What starts of small will multiply with each subsequent generation.<br>
Law of Binding and Loosing <span class="scripture-reference">Matt 16:19</span><br>
Whatever we bind on earth is bound in Heaven.<br>
When our ancestors committed sin, they bound all future generations to the consequences of that sin.

## Slide 10

SPIRITUAL INHERITANCE – Ex 1<br>
Adam and Eve  DISOBEDIENCE<br>
  Disobedience = original sin, fallen nature of all humanity<br>
  Generational transfer of good and evil:<br>
    Good = Blessing of mercy/acceptance in Abel<br>
    Evil = Curse of rebellion, idolatry, anger/rage, murder, and rejection in Cain<br>
    Good = Covenantal blessing of righteous and grace in Seth and his descendants (such as Enoch, Noah, Abraham, etc.)<br>
    Evil = Generational transfer of wickedness to Cain’s descendants (lewdness, depravity, polygamy, etc.)

**Speaker notes:**

Adam and Eve’s disobedience is the source or root of original sin and fallen nature in humanity.<br>
They passed both the curse of rebellion and the blessing of mercy to their sons:<br>
Cain chose to be rebellious and committed murder and idolatry; he passed that curse on to his children and subsequent generations<br>
Abel chose to obey God and received mercy and divine acceptance.
<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 5:19</span> – For by one man’s disobedience many were made sinners…
Santanic and demonic spirits then had access influence and corrupt the human nature, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 2:2</span> – Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according ot the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience.<br>
Generations of humanity then repositioned themselves to be aligned with the wrath and judgment of God, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:6</span>, Let no man deceive you with vain words: for because of these things cometh the wrath of God upon the children of disobedience. / <span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 3:6</span>, For which sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience
