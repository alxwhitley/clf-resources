Purposes of home meetings.

1\. Developing and facilitating community/family. They help facilitate community, common life together in Christ and by the Spirit.

You have to be around family to build family. Church is not a meeting but a way of life. People living out their relationships in Christ together on a daily basis.

2\. Facilitate and administrate mutual care for one another

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Corinthians 12:24-26</span> whereas our more presentable members have no need of it. But God has so composed the body, giving more abundant honor to that member which lacked, (25) so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same care for one another. (26) And if one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; if one member is honored, all the members rejoice with it.

<span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 6:2</span> Bear one another's burdens, and thereby fulfill the law of Christ.

They are points of contact to see how people are doing so we can see how to care for them. Where people know more intimately what you are going through on a regular basis.

3\. Provide an environment to minister (serve) and receive ministry (service).

You need to consider the interest of God and others ahead of your own interest.

<span class="scripture-reference">Philippians 2:3-4</span> NASB Do nothing from selfishness or empty conceit, but with humility of mind regard one another as more important than yourselves; 4 do not merely look out for your own personal interests, but also for the interests of others.

4\. They put us in a place of accountability.

Barna "Other large churches essentially become places where you can hide, where you don't get challenged, where you can feel more comfortable," he says. While the pollster says there may be a time and a place for that protection for people who have been "wounded through past church experiences," he says "there is a bigger picture at stake here.

You can only keep a cloke of façade so long when you are in regular contact with people.

5\. They help provide a setting where God’s word is worked out in our lives.

Often the main word to the body (mostly through Sunday morning messages) is amplified in the more intimate setting and common experience is shared in the application of the word in our lives. There is encouragement to pursue the application of the word.

As we share more intimately our experiences of everyday life together, others will help us reflect and evaluate things in light of God’s truth.

6\. They help create a relaxed, natural atmosphere which brings out spontaneous participation in edification and Spiritual gifting for the growth of all. The one anothers, Spiritual gifts etc.

The family must be intact. God does not intend for people to get married, have children and then divorce. Numerous studies conducted for a long time show us that broken families produce broken people.

They are built on Biblical love (<span class="scripture-reference">Eph. 5:25</span>). Biblical love, God type of love that is sacrificial.
