## Why the Word of God is Important:

## The Bible

1\. Is the soul authoritative point to define what we believe and how we live

2\. Speaks directly to us (we can apply it to our lives today)

3\. Defines truth and sin (else we would be given to our own ideas of truth)

4\. Shows us our need of salvation and God’s willingness to save

5\. Reveals Jesus Christ as Lord & Savior

6\. Reveal God – what brings God pleasure, what He loves, what He hates,

## His Intentions

7\. Is our sword – we can only skillfully use it if we interpret it rightly

## Divine Inspiration

1\. Second <span class="scripture-reference">Timothy 3:16</span>

a. inspired by God – God-breathed

b. useful/beneficial for teaching

c. reproof – describes the inward and outward experience of

acknowledging our sin; leads to repentance

d. correction - (epanorthosis) – “to set up straight again”; align with; to set on course

e. training in righteousness

Characteristics of the Word

1\. Effectual (Produces something)

a. Word of Life - <span class="scripture-reference">James 1:21</span>

Therefore putting aside all filthiness and all that remains of wickedness, in

humility receive the word implanted (set in firmly), which is able to save

your souls.

b. Shall not return empty/accomplishes a purpose <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 55:11</span>

So shall My word be which goes forth from My mouth; It shall not

return to Me empty, without accomplishing what I desire, and without

succeeding in the matter for which I sent it.

2\. Eternal

b. Eternal – <span class="scripture-reference">1 Peter 1:24-25</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 40:8</span>

The grass withers, the flower fades, but the word of our God stands forever.

3\. Alive

a. <span class="scripture-reference">John 1:1</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">John 1:14</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 4:12</span>

And the Word became flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld His

glory, glory as of the only begotten from the Father, full of grace and

truth.

For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two edged

sword, and piercing as far as both joints and marrow, and able to judge the

thoughts and intentions of the heart.

\*\*Ask Holy Spirit to speak to you/give you understanding/open your eyes to truth

(2) Reading

Chose a version that you can understand. (ESV, ASV, NIV study Bible)

Understand how you read/receive best. I learn best by writing things down. Write

it, read it, meditate on it

Read to get a good understanding of what is going on, what is being said, how it

applies to your life

(4) Memorizing

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 119:11</span> - “I have hidden your word in my heart

that I might not sin against you.”

When Jesus was tempted by Satan….It is written, he had scripture memorized,

Bad memory excuse – when you don’t train yourself to memorize, you forget. Ex. Knowing phone numbers, directions….cell phones/GPS systems

Sing it, speak it, write it, post it on your mirror….however you learn best
