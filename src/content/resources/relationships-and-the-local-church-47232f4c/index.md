## CHURCH LIFE CLASS

## RELATIONSHIPS AND THE LOCAL CHURCH

PREFACE: In its simplest form the church is relationships. It is walking out a joining together with God and those He joins us to in the Local Church. Relationships in the Local Church is not to be a

nice theory of fellowship but is to be a way of life serving God together.

### I. RELATIONSHIPS AND CHURCH IN LIGHT OF THE LOCAL CHURCH

A. The practical living of the truths of relationships and the Church involves a commitment to the Local Church.

Local Church - An identifiable group of believers who are joined together by God in a way of life

serving God under the oversight of God given pastors. This includes but is not limited to worship,

prayer, serving, fellowship, and mutual submission.

B. The context of the Bible (specifically the New Testament) is the context of the Local Church.

The New Testament was written to Local Churches and it is only in that context that it can be

properly understood.

The Church is mentioned 114 times in the New Testament, 96 of those times it is in reference to the

local Church.

Billy Graham in "Approaching Hoof beats" - "He (the apostle John, in Revelation) wrote to individual

churches, small clusters of believers, leaders and followers together. At the heart of these letters is

God's assumption that we belong together at work and at worship in a Local Church. I am convinced

that the cluster of believers of which you are a part, those brothers and sisters in Christ with whom

you join to pray and study, give and witness, is the basic unit through which God is working to

redeem the world."

Jerry White, Director of the Navigators - "Can one be a committed disciple and not be a part of a local

congregation? Practically, I believe the answer is no. If we take the Scriptures seriously, committed

discipleship must include fellowship, interaction, and functioning with other believers."

C. We tend to individualize the scriptures which causes us to misinterpret their Local Church context.

Examples: <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 12:1-2</span>, The context is vs 3-18

<span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 1:23</span>, The context is vs 22-2:10

You can read your Bible with individual glasses, or corporate glasses. It is obvious that the Bible

is a corporate book. It is not just God's personal love letter to you, but is His love letter to His

Bride, the Church.

D. To correctly experience the Biblical design of relationships there must be a joining together in a Local Church.

To say you are functioning right with the head (Christ) and are not functioning right with the body

(The Church) is an abnormality. Many believers want only a relationship with the head but not the

body, which is a distorted image of the plan of God.

## II. SUBMISSION IN RELATIONSHIPS

A. Walking together with other believers in submitted relationships is essential to the process of growth and maturity.

Relationships are the "school of the Spirit" through which God not only brings needed nourishment but also exposes are weaknesses to be dealt with which produces growth and maturity.

B. In God's scheme of things He has made us part of one another.

<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 12:5</span> 5 so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually members one of another. (NASB) Amplified "..mutually dependent on one another.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:25</span> 25 Therefore, laying aside falsehood, speak truth, each one of you, with his neighbor, for we are members of one another. (NASB)

There is a God designed need for the input of others in our lives.

C. There must be submission and humility in our relationships if we are to receive the nourishment from others in our lives.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:21</span> 21 and be subject to one another in the fear of Christ. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Peter 5:5</span> 5 Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble. (KJV)

Submit - to be subject, or put in subjection, to yield to power or authority of another, to defer to

another's judgment or opinion.

Subject - to rank under, to be in obedience, held in or bound by.

Subjection - to be open or exposed to the influence of others, allegiance to, being under the influence of.

D. Three important ideas about walking together in humble/submitted relationships.

1\. God has designed us in such a way that the influence and input of others in our lives is critical and used by God to help us grow.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:16</span> 16 from whom the whole body, being fitted and held together by that which every joint supplies, according to the proper working of each individual part, causes the growth of the body for the building up of itself in love. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 2:19</span> 19 and not holding fast to the head, from whom the entire body, being supplied and held together by the joints and ligaments, grows with a growth which is from God. (NASB)

F. Beware of an independent mindset.

This was the problem in <span class="scripture-reference">Col 2:16-23</span>.

These people are not following God's plan for His Church (vs 23). They have an appearance of wisdom

and elements of truth but are lacking the full picture of God's plan for His Church.

Independent people are not connected and are not receiving the vital input of others to help them

grow. This puts them in a dangerous position.

People of an independent mindset often display an emphasis on "My Ministry" without realizing

that ministry exists to further the needs of the Church.

Things that cause an independent mindset - lack of vision, pride, rebellion, covering of sin etc.
