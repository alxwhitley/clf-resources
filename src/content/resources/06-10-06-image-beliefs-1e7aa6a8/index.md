THE TRUTH ABOUT GOD<br>
Many of God’s children believe the lies of the devil and have decided that God does not love them. Most are oblivious to the chains that bind them.<br>
In His healing ministry, Jesus treated those who were hurting as victims and He set them free. Jesus came to release the brokenhearted and to set the captives free (Is. 61:1 and
<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 4:18</span>). His invitation to us is,
“... Come unto me ... and ye shall find rest unto your souls...” (Mat. 11:28-30).


Read the following scriptures and meditate on the truth of who God is until your image of<br>
God changes (<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 12:2</span>, “... be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind....”)


God is:<br>

## PRAYER TO HEAL MY GOD IMAGE

Lord, I know you created me in Your own image and likeness. I know I cannot receive

Your full blessings for me until I come to understand that You love me just the way I am

and that I do not have to earn Your blessings. Jesus died for us while we were yet sinners

so I know You love me in spite of myself.




Lord I repent for believing all the lies about who You are and who I am in You. I reject

and renounce them. I ask for Your forgiveness for any anger and resentment I have held

against You. I choose to give You Your rightful place in my heart and life.




Heal the false image I have had of You Lord, and change it to be an image of Your true

nature, that of love, compassion, healing, and blessings. Let me know without doubting

that I am accepted by You, one of Your special and precious children, in Jesus’ name.




Amen.

Healing the Broken Hearted: Foundational Beliefs

#3 FOUNDATIONAL BELIEFS

There are some foundational beliefs that every blood bought, Spirit-filled believer must have to live whole and free. He whom the Son sets free is free indeed. But we must be FULLY PERSUADED of the truth to live free. To oppress us, the enemy is often counting on our ignorance or unbelief.

Ponder and meditate on these scriptural truths. Write them down. Declare them. Believe them until you are fully persuaded!


1\. God created us in love. He knew us and set us apart BEFORE we were formed in our mother’s womb.<br>
(<span class="scripture-reference">Jeremiah 1:5</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 1:4-5</span>)<br>
2\. God created Adam and Eve in perfect health, in His own image and likeness, and gave them dominion over the earth (<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 1:26, 28</span>; 3 John 2). Sickness and disease did not exist in the Garden of Eden.<br>
3\. God made us as triune being – we are spirit, soul and body. Each part interacts with the other.<br>
4\. It is God’s will that our mind, will, emotions, spirit and body is healthy (3 John 2)<br>
5\. There are 2 kingdoms – Kingdom of God is one of love, acceptance, and forgiveness; The Kingdom of darkness is one of disobedience, rebellion, sin, sickness, separation, pain, torment, and so forth.<br>
6\. God is our peace, our hope and our deliverer. He will never leave us nor forsake us.<br>
7\. The completed work of Jesus Christ on the cross resulted in absolute and total victory over the kingdom of darkness. (<span class="scripture-reference">Rom 8:38-39</span>) Jesus has absolute authority in Heaven and Earth; ALL means ALL (<span class="scripture-reference">Matt 28:18</span>;
<span class="scripture-reference">Eph 1:21-22</span>)
8\. Jesus gave His power and authority to the 12 (<span class="scripture-reference">Matt 10:1</span>), to the 70 (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 10:9,19</span>), and to us (<span class="scripture-reference">Matt 16:17</span>-<br>
18). ALL has been given to us.<br>
9\. The only authority darkness has is what we give it. Whatever you come into agreement with you empower.<br>
10\. We are one with Christ. At salvation, we were unified with Christ. He is both with us and in us. Father, Son, and Holy Spirit live in us (Rom 6, 8; Eph 2,4; <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 2:20</span>)
