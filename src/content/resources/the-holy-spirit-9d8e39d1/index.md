## CHURCH LIFE CLASS

## THE HOLY SPIRIT

PREFACE: When a person has started a new life by faith in Christ, they are not left on their own

power to walk out this life. God has provided the power of the Holy Spirit to enable

believers to live and be witnesses for Him. In Acts 2 Peter speaks of the ministry of the

Holy Spirit in the last days. We are still living in that period of time and the ministry of the

Spirit continues. There has been much confusion about the person and work of the Spirit in

the life of the believer. In this study we hope to clear up some of the confusion on the work of the Spirit in the life of the believer.

## I. BIBLICAL PROOFS OF THE PERSONALITY OF THE SPIRIT.

A. The Holy Spirit is a personality, not a mere influence or power.

The Holy Spirit is spoken of as He. Jn 16:13,14

## 1\. Attributes of personality ascribed to Him:

1\. Knowledge - <span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 2:9-11</span> The Spirit knows the thoughts of God.

2\. Will - <span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 12:11</span> The Spirit distributes gifts according to God’s will.

3\. Mind - <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 8:27</span> Whoever knows the mind of the Spirit..., He intercedes for the saints.

4\. Love - <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:30</span> Paul urges the church by the love of the Spirit.

5\. Intelligence - <span class="scripture-reference">Neh 9:20</span> God gave the Spirit to instruct them.

6\. Grief - <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:30</span> Don’t grieve the Spirit

2\. Biblical Symbols for the Holy Spirit: These symbols often describe various operation of the Spirit.

1\. Fire - <span class="scripture-reference">Isa 4:4</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:4</span>; Mt 3:11

2\. Wind - Jn 3:8; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:2,3</span>

3\. Water - Jn 7:38,39; <span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 10:4</span>

4\. Oil - Mt 25:3; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 89:20</span>; 1Jn 2:27

5\. Dove - Mt 3:16

## II. BAPTISM IN THE HOLY SPIRIT.

A Questions that cause most of the controversy over this issue.

1\. Do we automatically get filled with the Spirit at salvation, or is there something more?

2\. Is there any accompanying manifestation in those who are filled with the Spirit?

3\. What is the procedure, or how is one filled with the Spirit?

4\. Is being filled with the Spirit a one time thing like water baptism?

In answering these questions we simply need to look at the Biblical accounts.

B. Biblical accounts.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:1-11</span> The Spirit fell on them while waiting in prayer before God Vs 1-4. The initial result

was speaking in tongues about the mighty deeds of God vs 4,11

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:14-17</span> Peter speaks of the ministry of the Spirit in the last days. He list manifestations of the

Spirit in the lives of believers with prophesy, dreams, and visions.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 4:8</span> The Scripture makes a distinction that Peter was filled with the Holy Spirit in speaking.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 4:31</span> The same believers who were filled in Acts 2 were filled again while praying. The result

here was speaking the world of God with boldness.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 6:3</span> When choosing men to serve, one requirement was that they were full of the Holy Spirit.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 8:12-24</span> A period of days elapsed between the time they received the word and were later filled

with the Spirit vs 12-17. They were filled with the Spirit as hands were laid on them vs 17.

Something observable happened as a result of them being filled with the he Spirit vs 18.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 9:1-17</span> A period of 3 days elapsed between Paul’s Damascus road experience and being filled

with the Spirit. He was filled with the Spirit as hands were laid on him vs 17-18. We do not

know what the initial manifestation of the Spirit was in Paul’s life but we do know that he later

operated in gifts of the Spirit, especially speaking in tongues <span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 14:1-40, 18</span>.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 10:43-48</span> The Holy Spirit fell on them while Peter was speaking. The initial manifestation was

speaking in tongues and exalting God vs 45-46. This event occurred before they were baptized

in water vs 47.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 19:1-6</span> Something in their experience gave Paul the indication that they hadn’t received the

Spirit when they initially believed vs 2. They were filled with the he Spirit as Paul laid his hands

on them vs 6. The initial manifestation was speaking in tongues and prophesying.

C. Answers from the Biblical accounts.

1\. There is no automatic assumption that one is filled at salvation.

2\. There is very clear accompanying manifestations when one is filled such as tongues, prophesy,

exalting God, and boldness. The most common manifestation is speaking in tongues. We should

expect accompanying manifestation when filled.

3\. People were filled at times when hands were laid on them, when praying, and when listening to

the word being preached.

4\. It is obvious that being filled with the Spirit is not a one time only thing. These accounts along

with the exhortation in <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 5:18</span> to be continually filled with the Spirit shows us that it is to be

continual experience.

D. Receiving the Spirit.

1\. Like everything else in God it is to be received by faith <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 3:2</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 10:17</span>.

2\. We must be convinced that the baptism of the Spirit is Biblical, necessary, applicable for today,

an experience distinct from salvation, and evidenced by speaking in tongues or other biblical

manifestations.

3\. Make Jesus your focus Mt 3:11.

4\. Ask for it based upon a hunger in your heart Lk 11:9-13; Jn 7:37-38

5\. Expect to receive, and act in faith to respond in Biblical manifestations. As in Acts they spoke in

tongues as the Spirit gave the utterance.
