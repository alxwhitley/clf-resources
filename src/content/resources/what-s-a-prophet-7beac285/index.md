## Old Testament Prophets

## 1\. The Definitions of a prophet – Major & Minor (separated by length of writing)

## Seer - Visionary

Prophet- The term prophet is derived from a Greek word meaning "to speak on behalf of."

Man of God

Servant of The Lord

There were two sets of prophets in the original Hebrew Bible. (Former & Later prophets)

The Former Prophets were – (Joshua, Judges, Samuel, Kings) – Latter are the Classical Prophetic Book

### The ways a Prophet heard God –

## Mental Picture-Visions,

Audible voice,

Experience-Trance or dream,

Immediate Unction – Spirit empowered or Lead

Number of Prophets in the Old Testament

65 Named Prophets (Abraham is title as the First Prophet)- <span class="scripture-reference">Gen 20:7</span>

## 14 Unnamed Prophets

22 bands or groups who Prophesied

4 Women labeled as Prophets (Miriam, Deborah, Huldah, Isaiah’s wife)

### The Callings of the Prophets varied-

Jeremiah called at Birth (Jer. 1)

Amos – called for a set time and purpose later in life. (Amos)

### 2\. Difference Between an O.T. Prophet and a N.T. Prophet

There are no more OT Prophets (Prophets of Judgment) – The OT Prophets were canonizing Prophets who touched a measure of the Grace of God to layout the Purposes of God. The Prophesied the very scriptures. – NT Prophets see in part to confirm the scripture or expound on it but not to create it- (they are limited by the laid plan of God by OT Prophets and NT scripture)

NT Prophets primary call is to Build the House of God and Prophecy Morality into the earth (mostly the present day Church) – All Prophecy is Redemptive – (<span class="scripture-reference">Heb 9:15</span>)

15 For this reason He is the mediator of a new covenant, so that, since a death has taken place for the redemption of the transgressions that were committed under the first covenant, those who have been called may receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.

OT Prophets were loners and outcast- They were greatly misunderstood and Feared

NT Prophets are called to function among the apostolic companies of men laying foundations of the church and the Kingdom.

### 3\. The Heart of the OT Prophet –

## Example:

The Glove and the Hand – (the Spirit came upon)- Literally means that the spirit would put the men of God on himself like a hand slides into a glove. The hand is the one moving but the glove is what we see. (So were the Prophets)

Spirit Came upon = OT: 3847- labash (law-bash'); or labesh (law-bashe'); (by implication) to put on a garment or clothe (oneself, or another), literally or figuratively- (in) apparel, arm, array (self), clothe (self), come upon, put (on, upon), wear.

The Prophets message often had the overview of a situation of which he had knowledge then he would deliver God’s perspective not Man’s perspective.

To common Society the state and Moral corruption of the day seemed Normal but to the Prophetic eye it seemed Dreadful

The Prophets Life was a divine witness of the God of Abraham and His words Gods Testimony.

Prophets were often critics of their societies, and where they were successful, they were reformers. The preaching of the prophets usually had to do with justice and morality, calling on their audiences to mend their ways.

The prophets insisted that God prefers upright and ethical behavior over slavish devotion to details of worship services.

Prophets weren’t as interested with the current affairs of man and nature as they were the history of the Future

4\. The Function of the OT Prophet –

The prophets were not mere Mouth Pieces for God but a Person, Not just an Instrument, a Partner with God. The Prophet became the very words of God in a moment.

The Prophet became the very Heart and emotions of God in a moment. He does not merely speak for God but becomes the very voice of God in the moment.

The Prophet Not only conveyed the words of God but He Revealed the attitude of God.

The Prophet seemed to do unto others what God had done unto him. They could demand total devotion and humbleness of heart because they had first been shattered by the God the walked with and spoke for.

The Prophets Addressed – Kings, Priest, False Prophets, entire nations

The Prophets language was a Fusion of contradictions. – The language can be luminous & Explosive, firm and contingent, harsh and compassionate.

5\. The Design of the Prophets words

The prophets words were like a Sharp Sword in the hand of a skilled Bearer and like a Polished arrow shot straight from the mouth of God

The prophets Words were often

Stern

Stinging

Sour

Full of cold tones of Judgment

(Yet behind every word was the undying Love of God and his Compassion for Mankind to return to Him.)

Only Spoke if the lord was speaking (If I have nothing to say you have nothing to Say)

<span class="scripture-reference">Isa 50:4</span> "The Lord GOD has given Me The tongue of the learned,That I should know how to speak A word in season to him who is weary.He awakens Me morning by morning,He awakens My ear To hear as the learned.

In OT Days many lands had Prophets- Most of the Time they were the counselors of Kings.

Gods were idolized in every Land of the day around Israel – Located in the center of The Trade routes the Israelites were pressed and subjected to many foreign practices.

The More Powerful a King or Nation the more Powerful was there God. If a nation was defeated – they would destroy their gods

The Prophets declared that they Gods of foreign nations were an abomination before God. And that the nations should serve the one true God.

The Goal of the prophets was to turn the wayward nation of Judah and Israel back to relationship with God. There Prophecy was to Change the Inner man and conquer the Callousness of ones Heart!
