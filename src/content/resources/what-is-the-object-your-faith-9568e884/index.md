## WHAT IS THE OBJECT OF YOUR FAITH?

saying: 2 "I am the LORD your God, who<br>
brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the<br>
house of bondage. 3 "You shall have no other<br>

Introduction<br>
Life has a way of confronting us with hard<br>
questions that requires a sober state of mind to<br>
handle it.<br>
The queen of sheba had serious observations<br>
based on several questions that were unsettled<br>
in her heart.


1 Kings 10: 1 Now when the queen of Sheba heard of<br>
the fame of Solomon concerning the name of the<br>
LORD, she came to test him with hard questions. 2<br>
She came to Jerusalem with a very great retinue,<br>
with camels that bore spices, very much gold, and<br>
precious stones; and when she came to Solomon,<br>
she spoke with him about all that was in her heart. 3<br>
So Solomon answered all her questions; there was<br>
nothing so difficult for the king that he could not<br>

the food on his table, the seating of his servants, the service of his waiters and their apparel, his cupbearers, and his entryway by which he went up to the house of the LORD, there was no more spirit in her. 6 Then she said to the king: "It was a true report which I heard in my own land about your words and your wisdom.


7 "However I did not believe the words until I<br>

"Happy are your men and happy are these your<br>

9 "Blessed be the LORD your God, who delighted in<br>

Few questions to consider.<br>
1\. What really makes you feel secure?<br>
2\. What’s the object of your fears or uncertainties?<br>
3\. What motivates your Christianity?<br>
4\. What drives you to pray, give, read the word, serve<br>
God?<br>
5\. What is the object that describes your significance or gives you a sense of importance?<br>
6\. What is the source of your peace and Joy?<br>
7\. What generates your value?


The questions above if not given the right<br>
perspective in getting answers, will always lead<br>
us to have an imbalanced life that technically<br>
makes us unstable.<br>
Where ignorance is, weakness is inevitable<br>
Ignorance therefore becomes the foundation of<br>
your darkness and light disappears.


What do you need for you to constructively build your<br>
life?<br>
1\. Genuinely and unashamedly increase your level of<br>
WISDOM, KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING<br>
Proverbs 24: 3 Through wisdom a house is built, And by<br>
understanding it is established; 4 By knowledge the rooms are filled With all precious and pleasant riches.<br>
5 A wise man is strong, Yes, a man of knowledge<br>
increases strength; 6 For by wise counsel you will wage your own war, And in a multitude of counselors there is safety.


2\. Build capacity to accommodate the variables of life.<br>
James 1: 2 My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials,3 knowing that the testing of your faith produces patience.4 But let patience have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking nothing.<br>

tomorrow go out against them, for the LORD is with you."
