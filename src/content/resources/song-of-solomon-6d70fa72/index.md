## Old Testament Survey- Song of Solomon

## Author:

Solomon

## Symbolism:

Displays Jehovah’s Love for Israel and Christ Love for the church.

## Overview:

Solomon visits his vineyards in Lebanon and comes a cross a Shulamite women. He tries to win here heart but she runs off. So Solomon dresses up like a shepherd and approaches her and eventually they begin to fall in love. So Solomon claims her as his wife and returns for her and reveals to her that he is the king and she goes back to the palace with him.
