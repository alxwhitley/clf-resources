## B.) WHO SHOULD TAKE THE LORD'S SUPPER?

### Only those who are already Christians. <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 14:22-26</span>

For anyone who eats and drinks without recognizing the body of the Lord eats and drinks judgment upon himself. <span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 11:29</span>

I. Why do I need to keep the Act of The Lord’s Supper?

Communion was a commandment revealed to the Apostle Paul, and was instituted by the Lord Jesus Himself at Passover (Last Supper)

Always points you to the Cross, Death, Burial, and Resurrection of Christ (Due in remembrance)

The place of fresh evaluation of the Cross and commitment in your personal life with God. (Don’t take the Lords Cup unworthily)

The place of fellowship and cleansing- (<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 10:19-22</span>)
