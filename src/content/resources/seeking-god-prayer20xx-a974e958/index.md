Seasons of Seeking: Finding and Drawing Close to God with Purpose

Prayer: The Action of Seeking / The Avenue of Pursuit

Introduction: In this final lesson on seeking God, prayer is the focus. As with the previous lesson where we focused on the Word of God, this is not a redundant exposition on prayer as a devotional admonishment or religious duty—rather this is a consideration of prayer as joyful conclusion and hopeful/hope-filled means to enhancing your personal relationship God; prayer is an opportunity to pour out your hear to God, to be real with God and also to partner with Him.

<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 11:6</span>, But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

 One of the most diligent means of seeking God is through prayer.

<span class="scripture-reference">Psalms 32:6</span>, For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found: surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him.



## Basic Review:

 Understand prayer is a critical part of your relationship with God. Any relationship will be stalled where communication is diminished or lacking.<br>
 Prayer is about relationship, not about being religious. Prayer is about fellowship and entertaining the presence of God, not about duty or marking of a spiritual task list.<br>
 Prayer was taught by Jesus as a pattern of seeking God, which included:<br>
o Reverence and relationship with God as Father<br>
o Declaration and focus on the Kingdom, ushering in God’s rule and reign in one’s personal life and in the earth.<br>
o Unreserved embrace of God’s will that leads to transformation in society and cultures on the earth.
