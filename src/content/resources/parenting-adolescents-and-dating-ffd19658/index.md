## Parenting Adolescents

## Generation Gaps – Teenage Sub Culture

Where did the contemporary phenomenon of the idea of “teenager” (isolated from parents and adults by generation gaps) come from?

It didn’t exist prior to World War II. If you read older publications or talk with people living before WW II teenagers were often referred to as boys, girls, kids, young people, adolescents, and youths.

In the 1934 Webster’s dictionary - only the adjective “teen-age” is listed. It was a word used simply to describe someone in a particular stage in life: a “teen-age” girl, as opposed to a “middle-age” man. Not until his 1961 Webster’s is “teenager” used as a noun. So what happened during those 27 years that changed society’s view of adolescents?

The idea of “teenager” came into being as youths, over a period of time, drifted further and further from adults. As they were left to spend more and more unsupervised time with their peers, an entire subculture was born with its own styles and music. Even the increasing availability of the automobile helped further transport youths away from older generations to be with their peers. You could say that the separation of the generations helped create teenagers.

Look at the classic films of James Dean, especially in “Rebel Without A Cause”, where “Jimmy” had to face his life’s trials alone due to his contumacious mother and helpless father. Also, books like “Catcher in the ” where the character Salinger taught young readers to trust no one over 14. Rock and Roll music coming on the scene in the 50s became the music of the “teenage subculture.” I even read a quote attributed to rocker David Crosby expressing how some have made it their goal to rip parents and teens apart:

“I figured that the only thing to do was steal their kids. I still think it’s the only thing to do. By saying that, I’m not talking about kidnapping; I’m just talking about changing people’s value systems, which removes them from their parents’ world very effectively.”

B. One of the biggest challenges our adolescents face that can sidetrack them from pursuing God's goal

There are two great forces which combined create havoc during adolescence and make it such a

powerfully dangerous time.

1) Hormones - Science and experience tells us that there is tremendous biochemical changes that occur during puberty. Much like severe premenstrual syndrome in females. It affects their personality, their drives, desires, etc.

The whole issue of male female relationships is connected to both of these things.

II. Casual Dating in our society.

Casual - Dictionary - happening by chance, without definite or serious intention, uncertain.

A. How the casual dating game works in our society.

You see someone of the opposite sex you feel a romantic or lustful attraction towards, so you arrange

a time and setting to spend close intimate time together.

Much like our relationship with cars. We see a car we are attracted to we want to figure out a way to

ride in it. Test drive, lease it, or buy it.

The usual course of a casual dating relationship - Go date the person you are attracted to for a period of time until you get tired of them, don't feel attracted to them any more, or you find someone you are

more attracted to. Can be big problems with this "breaking up is hard to do"

B. Reasons our society gives for casual dating.

1\. Getting to know members of the opposite sex. It provides a setting for this

2\. Companionship/social companionship. Certain types of events it is more socially acceptable to have a companion of the opposite sex at them.

3\. Help us find the person we want to marry. If we date enough people we will get an idea of the type

of person we want to marry, and find the right one.

4\. Peer pressure. Dating, and having a boyfriend and girlfriend, helps you fit in better with your peers.

5\. Hormone stimulation and fulfillment. The desire to stimulate and fulfill the romantic and sensual capacities in our lives.

Probably the most predominate reason is Number 4 and 5.

VII. Parenting Adolescence.

A. Understand the nature of the adolescent time of life.

There are three great forces which combined create havoc during the adolescent time of life

1) Hormones - Science and experience tells us that there is tremendous biochemical changes

that occur during puberty. Much like the premenstrual syndrome in females.

2\. The social awakening - the influence of the peer group becomes the most important factors in their

life. They move from the parents being the dominant influence to their peers.

3\. Intellectual shift - They become more intelligent and see things different. They begin to think more on their own. They form opinions because problem solving techniques are growing.

Adolescent period of time is much like the tension in the early space program. When the spaceship

enters the atmosphere it is very dangerous and you lose contact with it for 7 minutes. For that time you don't know if they are going to make it or not. They are going through a period of tremendous

dangerous heat.

B. Getting the monkey off the back of parents.

We live in an age where parents have been hamstrung by the idea of determinism in our society. It

in some ways has become a plague on parenting..

Determinism - All behavior is caused, a child chooses nothing, he merely responds to experiences.

The rise in the psychological field of so called experts tend to want to place the parents in one way

or another as the cause of anything that can conceivably go wrong with children.

Our society has gotten caught up in this idea of blame. People keep busy in an illusion of trying to

always know the cause of something which seems to lessen responsibility for facing our wrong behavior. Making everyone feel like helpless victims is unproductive.

Things parents of adolescents seem to constantly battle with.

-Guilt. What have I done? If I could only do it over again.

-Fear. What if I can't keep them away from that crowd, or activity?

-Apathy. They are on their own now. Kids are going to sow their wild oats, we just need to let them

do it.

-Hopelessness. Its too late, there is nothing more I can do.

-Divorced parent syndorme. We have already caused enough pain in our children because of the

divorce. I don't want to make thier life any more miserable by being too strict.

Things that parents need to realize to get the monkey off their backs.

-Parents are people too. We are not perfect and make mistakes. This is not an effort to excuse our

mistakes but to realize our imperfections and not be hamstrung by the perfect parent syndrome.

We need to operate in the Biblical directives when we make mistakes, confession and forgiveness,

and then get back to the task of parenting.

-Realize your limitations. We all want the best possible life for our children yet we can't get caught up in the super parent syndrome. Parents material and emotional resources are limited. Our young

people need to learn this. The best clothes are not the fundamental right or a necessity.

-You are the parent and teens are not. You are the parent and the manager of your home. God's

authority in the home is with the parents. It is in the nature of children to challenge authority but

that is no cause for you to become insecure. Parents have different roles than children. Parents are

in charge. Don't allow yourself to get into manipulative arguments with your teens that go

nowhere. You, not your teens have been given the authority and have the wisdom to judge in your home.

In this chapter we read again of the fearful consequences of a neglected religious education. Eli’s sons were wicked: their father knew the Lord; but he neither taught his children, nor restrained them by his parental authority. I have already had occasion to remark, that were a proper line of conduct pursued in the education of children, how few profligate sons and daughters, and how few broken-hearted parents should we find! The neglect of early religious education, connected with a wholesome and affectionate restraint, is the ruin of millions. Many parents, to excuse their indolence and most criminal neglect, say, “We cannot give our children grace.” What do they mean by this? That God, not themselves, is the author of the irregularities and viciousness of their children. They may shudder at this imputation: but when they reflect that they have not given them right precepts, have not brought them under firm and affectionate restraint; have not showed them, by their own spirit, temper, and conduct, how they should be regulated in theirs; when either the worship of God has not been established in their houses, or they have permitted their children, on the most trifling pretenses, to absent themselves from it; when all these things are considered, they will find that, speaking after the manner of men, it would have been a very extraordinary miracle indeed if the children had been found preferring a path in which they did not see their parents conscientiously tread. Let those parents who continue to excuse themselves by saying, “We cannot give grace to our children,” lay their hand on their conscience, and say whether they ever knew an instance where God withheld his grace, while they were, in humble subserviency to him, performing their duty. The real state of the case is this: parents cannot do God’s work, and God will not do theirs; but if they use the means, and train up the child in the way he should go, God will not withhold his blessing.

It is not parental fondness, nor parental authority, taken separately, that can produce this beneficial effect. A father may be as fond of his offspring as Eli, and his children be sons of Belial; he may be as authoritative as the grand Turk, and his children despise and plot rebellion against him. But let parental authority be tempered with fatherly affection; and let the rein of discipline be steadily held by this powerful but affectionate hand; and there shall the pleasure of God prosper; there will he give his blessing, even life for evermore. Many fine families have been spoiled, and many ruined, by the separate exercise of these two principles. Parental affection, when alone, infallibly degenerates into foolish fondness; and parental authority frequently degenerates into brutal tyranny when standing by itself. The first sort of parents will be loved without being respected; the second sort will be dreaded, without either respect or esteem. In the first case obedience is not exacted, and is therefore felt to be unnecessary, as offenses of great magnitude pass without punishment or reprehension: in the second case, rigid exaction renders obedience almost impossible; and the smallest delinquency is often punished with the extreme of torture, which, hardening the mind, renders duty a matter of perfect indifference.

Parents, lay these things to heart: remember Eli and his sons; remember the dismal end of both! Teach your children to fear God, use wholesome discipline, be determined, begin in time, mingle severity and mercy together in all your conduct, and earnestly pray to God to second your godly discipline with the power and grace of his Spirit.

Education is generally defined that series of means by which the human understanding is gradually enlightened, and the dispositions of the heart are corrected, formed, and brought forth, between early infancy and the period when a young person is considered as qualified to take a part in active life. Whole nations have been corrupted, enfeebled, and destroyed, through the want of proper education: through this multitudes of families have degenerated; and a countless number of individuals have come to an untimely end. Parents who neglect this, neglect the present and eternal interests of their offspring.

C. Suggestions for raising adolescents

1\. Take advantage of the community support of the Church. God has placed in the church many

mothers and fathers. We need to support, encouragement, counsel, and admonishment of our

brothers and sisters in the Lord. We also need other parents who help us with our teens when

we are not around. That is why we need to work together.

2\. Boredom is dangerous to energetic teenagers, keep them moving.

Don't be afraid to have them serve.

3\. Don't be afraid to require responsibility in the family.

Your home is the first place your teens learn important concepts of family in the Kingdom. Parents need to insist that all members cooperate for the good of the family. Doing your own thing based

on your own self interest is anti Kingdom and anti family. In your home your teens learn the

Biblical principle of losing your life for Christ sake in order to find.

It gives them self esteem to accomplish things and learn to do things well

Make it a priority to build relationships with your teen even though it might seem impossible.

on one with them taking an interest in their life, thoughts, and feelings.

Express your love to them even if it seems like they don't receive it. The Biblical principal of

accepting and loving one another like Christ does to us is important to keep in mind here. Jesus

constantly expressed His love and desire for relationship with mankind. Let them know they are

special to you.

7\. Be firm with tough cases.

You don't need to cower down to your strong willed child. Quit apologizing.

Anguishing, guilt-ridden parents become hamstrung. Love often is often tough.

There was a permissive method of child rearing that originated in the 40s with writings of people such as Benjamin Spock. It blossomed in the 50s, and in the 60s it gained total respectability. This is

the legacy of parenting on the Baby boomers.

Parenting isn't for cowards. We need to make demands on our teens and stick with them in spite of

all their defiant accusations. Be firm yet in control.

You are the boss and the sooner and younger your children understand this the better.
