Session 9: Praise, Worship, Prayer, & Prophetic (Chapter 16, pp. 39-45 & Chapter 18, pp. 53-58)

Praise and Worship.

Praise is the joyful response of all creatures to God’s greatness (<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 150:6</span>).

Worship is the tender and intimate response of God’s children to His fatherhood and majesty (<span class="scripture-reference">John 4:23</span>)

Worship belongs to God and includes a myriad of expressions, such as lifting our hands, clapping, shouting/dancing, playing instruments, & singing (Ps. 150)

Prayer

Prayer is intentional communication with God, which deepens intimacy and fosters relationship with Him.

Prayer is dynamic and includes various aspects such as confession, worship, meditation, petition, thanksgiving, intercession, praying in the Spirit, and can be coupled with fasting (see pages 55-56 for verse references).

Prayer should be engaged without ceasing, thus it can be spontaneous or scheduled and regardless of structure, it must be sincere (<span class="scripture-reference">1 Thessalonians 5:17</span>).

Prophetic

Being prophetic is the faith-filled response and nature of following the Spirit of God as He leads—as a local church, corporate family, and individual believers, this looks like seeking God specifically for His will, ways, and word in any and every situation, which when discerned, heard, and obeyed releases kingdom manifestations and impact. (<span class="scripture-reference">2 Corinthians 5:7</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 8:14</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 5:18</span>).

As prophetic people we are willing to release, receive, weigh, and judge prophetic words; we make space for honorable prophetic ministry that draws people closer to Jesus (e.g., prophecy, prophetic singing, prophetic exhortation, use of prophetic gifts tongues/interpretation, words of knowledge/wisdom, prayers of deliverance/breakthrough) (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 10:31</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">1 Corinthians 12:7-11</span>).

As prophetic people we honor and receive authentic 5-fold ministry gifts of apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers, recognizing their grace to strengthen local churches and edify believers. (<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:11-13</span>)
