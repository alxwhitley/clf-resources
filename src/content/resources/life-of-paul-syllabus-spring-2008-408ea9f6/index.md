DESCRIPTION: A course of study in the Life of Paul as outlined in the Book of Acts. His epistles, other New Testament books and other historical documents will provide a context and backdrop from which to study Paul’s life and its’ effects.

PURPOSE: To train the student of the Bible in the chronology of Paul’s life, emphasizing the important role of the Holy Spirit. In addition the major theological themes of Paul’s life and ministry will be explored and how all of this can help us become better disciples

Late homework will be accepted, but a grade penalty will be given. No late work will be accepted beyond the final without a reasonable excuse.

## STUDENT EVALUATION:

Your grade in the Life of Paul class will be determined by the following factors and assignments:

Three research papers. Each will be a minimum of three (3) pages typed, double spaced (12 pt).

The first paper will be a book report from one of the “Diary” series books by Gene Edwards. \[Due Feb. 25th\]

The second paper will be on the letter to the Ephesians, with Bibliography. You will address two things in your paper:

The historical background of the letter and the Ephesian church.

Pauline ecclesiology as found in Ephesians.

The third paper will be on “What I have learned about being a disciple who builds the church from Paul’s life”.

Grading of paper: 25% grammar and mechanics, 25% presentation & 50% content.

Homework – as given.

Quizzes – as announced
