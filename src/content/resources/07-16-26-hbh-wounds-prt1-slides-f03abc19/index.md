Understand Your Family of Origin<br>
 The family unit that you grew up in is your

family of origin.

 Often includes parents and siblings, but also is

inclusive of other relatives or people who lived<br>
with you during part of your childhood.

 We are strongly influenced in who we become

by those who raised us and the significant<br>
others within that family group.


COMMON SOUL WOUNDS<br>
 Father wounds and Mother wounds are those

inflicted by our primary caregivers and/or biological<br>
parents.

 Healing of such parental wounding often corrects

how we see God as Heavenly Father and how we<br>
receive Him as El Shaddai (<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 17:1</span>, the God who<br>
is strong enough to help and sensitive enough to<br>
care), our Ultimate Caregiver.


Father Wounds<br>
 Fathers are meant to provide, protect,

and give identity.

 What happens when a father is

negligent, absent, or abusive?

 Father wounds can produce a lack of

security, identity, or provision, which<br>
may allow entrance of an orphan spirit<br>
or normalized feelings of rejection and<br>
abandonment.

Mother Wounds<br>
 Mothers are meant to nurture, comfort,<br>
and teach.<br>
 What happens when a mother is<br>
negligent, absent, or abusive?<br>
 Mothers wounds can cause<br>

affection, which often leads to<br>
insecurity and a subconscious or<br>
desperate search for belonging,<br>
comfort, and connection.

DEALING WITH SINFUL RESPONSES<br>
TO FATHER/MOTHER WOUNDS<br>
 The effects and impacts of parental wounding are often made worse by the sinful responses of a son/daughter.<br>
 In addition to asking Jesus to heal the wound, one must also take responsibility for any sinful response:
1) Repentance – <span class="scripture-reference">John 5:14, 8</span>:11
2) Releasing Bitterness – <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 12:15</span>

FORGIVENESS IS CRITICAL<br>
Forgiveness is not optional; as a child of God YOU NEED to forgive others.

1\. It is in the nature and redemptive plan of God to forgive humanity.<br>
(<span class="scripture-reference">Ps 86:5</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 103:2-4</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Jer 31:34</span>)

2\. As an act of faith and in accordance with the principals of sowing and reaping, God’s children are to continually practice forgiving others (<span class="scripture-reference">Hos 10:12</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 6:7-10</span>; Mat 18:21-22)

3\. Frees the one extending forgiveness from torment (Mat 18:21-35)


UNDERSTAND FORGIVENESS<br>
3\. Forgiveness is the willful act of releasing those who have sinned against us; in forgiveness we release the individual from owing us anything and free them from being obligated to us in accordance with their sin.<br>
(<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 6:12, 2</span> <span class="scripture-reference">Cor 2:10</span>)

4\. Forgiveness DOES NOT

1\. Justify or excuse sinful behavior (Ezekiel18:20)

2\. Result or require automatic reconciliation of relationship (Numbers<br>
12:9-15)

3\. Dismiss the need for maintaining clear, healthy boundaries when necessary (<span class="scripture-reference">Amos 3:3</span>)
