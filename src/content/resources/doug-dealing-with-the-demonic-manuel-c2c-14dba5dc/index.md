Dealing with the Demonic

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Peter 5:8-9</span> NASB Be of sober spirit, be on the alert. Your adversary, the devil, prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour. (9) But resist him, firm in your faith

A manual to help believers effectively resist the demonic in their lives and the lives of others

## Doug Kreighbaum

Dealing with the Demonic

2Co 2:11 so that no advantage would be taken of us by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.

Two errors God’s people fall into when approaching the whole issue of the demonic.

1\. A liberal camp that either believes Satan doesn’t really exist and Biblical examples of the demonic are simply primitive man expressing myths.

2\. The "charismaniac" camp that become extremely preoccupied with the devil. In their zeal they see “a demon behind every bush.”

We are often plagued by our western worldview

The western worldview tends to be a scientific, rational view of life that marginalizes the supernatural. In other places in the world, among people with a different worldview, there is a greater understand that there is a spiritual world containing both good and demonic powers that directly affect our natural world. The worldview presented in the Bible readily accepts the reality of a demonic realm that affects the natural realm we live in.

Demonic in the Old Testament

There is a clear assumption in the OT that demons existed and troubled God’s people. Satan was found to be behind the idolatry of the nations (<span class="scripture-reference">Ps 106:36-37</span>: 109:6). Satan worked, through the serpent, to cause man to fall into the grip of sin. From the fall of man came the basic foundation of God's entire redemptive plan; that of Jesus Christ dying to triumph over Satan and draw man back into relationship with God. Throughout the Old Testament evil spirits are always seen as being in abeyance to the Father God. Satan appears, with the "sons of God", to accuse Job and request permission to put Job to the test. It is interesting to note that God initiated the discussion about Job, and decided all the parameters in which Satan could work. One of the biggest lies that Satan has been able to perpetuate through the centuries is that there is a battle between good and evil. In reality Satan poses no threat to God and works completely within the boundaries set out by Almighty God.

Demonic in the New Testament

The existence of a literal devil, or Satan, is mentioned by every author of the New Testament. Nineteen books mention him by name and eight mention the existence of demons or evil angels. All together there are over 250 references in the New Testament to evil spirits or demons. There are 29 references to Satan in the gospels, and in 25 of these, Jesus is speaking about him as a personal being. Jesus both healed the sick and cast out demons (Mk. 1:32) Demonism is distinguished from both epilepsy (Mt. 4:24) and sickness and leprosy (Mt. 10:8). Jesus could command demons out with a word <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 1:27</span> NASB They were all amazed, so that they debated among themselves, saying, "What is this? A new teaching with authority! He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him.”

Christ Ministry and the demonic

Christ ministry of redeeming man back to God was also linked to redeeming man from the grip of sin and the demonic. Any attempt of thinking it is one without the others is deficient.

<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 1:39</span> NASB And He went into their synagogues throughout all Galilee, preaching and casting out the demons.

<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 1:34</span> NASB And He healed many who were ill with various diseases, and cast out many demons; and He was not permitting the demons to speak, because they knew who He was.

<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 3:11</span> NASB Whenever the unclean spirits saw Him, they would fall down before Him and shout, "You are the Son of God!"

Later on in Christ's ministry He sent out the 70 and the 12 to go and heal the sick and cast out demons. <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 10:17</span> NASB The seventy returned with joy, saying, "Lord, even the demons are subject to us in Your name." Mt 9-11.

After Christ's death, resurrection and ascension Peter describes the Lords ministry; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 10:38</span> NASB "You know of Jesus of Nazareth, how God anointed Him with the Holy Spirit and with power, and how He went about doing good and healing all who were oppressed by the devil, for God was with Him.

<span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 61:1-3</span> puts the entire ministry of Jesus into perspective:

<span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 61:1-3</span> NASB The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me, Because the LORD has anointed me To bring good news to the afflicted; He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, To proclaim liberty to captives And freedom to prisoners; (2) To proclaim the favorable year of the LORD And the day of vengeance of our God; To comfort all who mourn, (3) To grant those who mourn in Zion, Giving them a garland instead of ashes, The oil of gladness instead of mourning, The mantle of praise instead of a spirit of fainting. So they will be called oaks of righteousness, The planting of the LORD, that He may be glorified

In this context we see a model for our ministry as we walk with Jesus

1) Proclamation; 2) Counseling and praying for healing; 3) deliverance from enemies; 4) praising and worshipping; and 5) teaching.

Any ministry that leaves out any of these areas will eventually find itself deficient. The key to walking in it comes from Jesus words.

<span class="scripture-reference">John 5:30</span> NASB "I can do nothing on My own initiative. As I hear, I judge;

<span class="scripture-reference">John 14:10</span> NASB The words that I say to you I do not speak on My own initiative, but the Father abiding in Me does His works.

The Christian and the demonic

Believers are not to be ignorant of the demonic

<span class="scripture-reference">2 Corinthians 2:11</span> NASB so that no advantage would be taken of us by Satan, for we are not ignorant of his schemes.

Christians are not to be ignorant of satanic schemes nor are we to be cowardly. We need to be able to recognize the enemy’s tactics. We are called to be soldiers, stationed behind enemy lines,

seeking to save those that are lost by sharing the good news of the gospel. Finding prisoners, we are to release them from the shackles of bondage to the enemy.

<span class="scripture-reference">Revelation 21:7-8</span> NASB "He who overcomes will inherit these things, and I will be his God and he will be My son. (8) "But for the cowardly and unbelieving and abominable and murderers and immoral persons and sorcerers and idolaters and all liars, their part will be in the lake that burns with fire and brimstone, which is the second death.

The design of the demonic is to keep us from going forward in Christ. Many people who come to Christ realize that they seem to have more struggles since they became a Christian than before. This is often the shock of spiritual warfare. We were not much of a threat to the demonic kingdom before Christ. Now as we serve Jesus we are. Before Christ God reached out in love to woo us to Jesus. Since coming into Christ the enemy works as Jesus said, “to kill, steal, and destroy.”

<span class="scripture-reference">John 10:10</span> NASB "The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy; I came that they may have life, and have it abundantly.

The War concerning the demonic

We live in a world that due to the failure first with Adam and Eve lies under bondage to the demonic and evil. <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 8:22</span> NASB For we know that the whole creation groans and suffers the pains of childbirth together until now. The Old Testament prophets all looked forward to, and anticipated a day when God would rid the world of evil and establish His kingdom on earth (<span class="scripture-reference">Amos 9:13-15</span>; Is 65:17).

Jesus came to usher in that age and the day in which the world will be rid of evil and the demonic. The heart of the work of Christ in reconciling man and enabling them to overcome is the destruction of the works of the devil. <span class="scripture-reference">1 John 3:8</span> NASB the one who practices sin is of the devil; for the devil has sinned from the beginning. The Son of God appeared for this purpose, to destroy the works of the devil.

Jesus work on the cross was step one and the salvation of each Christian and their overcoming sin and the demonic are part of the steps leading to the final day of victory. <span class="scripture-reference">Philippians 2:10-11</span> NASB so that at the name of Jesus EVERY KNEE WILL BOW, of those who are in heaven and on earth and under the earth, (11) and that every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 16:20</span> NASB The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet. The grace of our Lord Jesus be with you.

The demonic opposes everything that will lead to that final day in order to delay it.

<span class="scripture-reference">2 Timothy 2:24-26</span> NASB The Lord's bond-servant must not be quarrelsome, but be kind to all, able to teach, patient when wronged, (25) with gentleness correcting those who are in opposition, if perhaps God may grant them repentance leading to the knowledge of the truth, (26) and they may come to their senses and escape from the snare of the devil, having been held captive by him to do his will.

Until salvation the enemy focuses on keeping people in darkness <span class="scripture-reference">2 Corinthians 4:4</span>. He does by shielding the mind from the seeds of truth or chocking them out when they come <span class="scripture-reference">Matt 13:3-20</span>. The enemy also plants tares (false things) in the world to hinder and reduce the Kingdom crop’s yield <span class="scripture-reference">Matt 13:24-30</span>.

Believer, individually and corporately are opposed by the enemy because they are a threat. We are the body of Christ through which He acts in the earth. Persecution is another byproduct the enemy uses Jn. 15:20.

Another reason we are attacked is that we are vulnerable to it. There is a ground inside of us that is open to attack. It is the battlefield we fight on.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:26-27</span> MKJV Be angry, and do not sin. Do not let the sun go down upon your wrath, (27) neither give place to the Devil.

The idea of “place” literally means a definite specific place or territory. The word translated place, "topos", is used 84 times in the Bible; in 81 of these the implication is a literal physical place. The place Paul is talking about then is a specific area that Satan can gain control of and use as headquarters to begin to attack other areas.

Influence of the demonic in the life of the Christian – a systematic approach

1\. The Greek word translated "demon possessed" throughout the New Testament "daimonizomenos" does not mean owned by a demon (possession), but simply "demonized" or exercised by a demon. The phrase " daimonizomai " can be most accurately translated "to be under the power of a demon” (Thayer), “to be exercised by a demon” (Strongs),” or “to be demon possessed” (NASEC). In that sense it basically describes the condition of a person who is inhabited by demons, or be under the control of to various degrees resulting in a range of symptoms.

Being caught in the snare of the devil speaks of "being held captive to do his \[Satan's\] will". When we are cleansing our vessel, the reference is to removal of that which dishonors \[abstain from wickedness (vs 19)\]. In context, we are to remove that which is within' the house.

The issue of our standing/place in Christ and the demonic influence

As believers we have a new place and standing in Christ. As we walk in that place and see what we have in Christ walked out more and more in our life the result will be to take away the places that have been given to the demonic.

Joh 14:30 "I will not speak much more with you, for the ruler of the world is coming, and he has nothing in Me;

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 4:13</span> NASB When the devil had finished every temptation, he left Him until an opportune time.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:27</span> MKJV neither give place to the Devil.

At conversion we receive positional victory over Satan and his kingdom (<span class="scripture-reference">Col 2:15</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 8:37-39</span>). Nothing can ever again separate us from the love of Christ; but, though we are positional conquerors, we must resist experientially from that position (Eph. 6). In the same way that we are positionally free from sin (Rom. 6) each of us is in a continual battle to "reckon" ourselves dead to sin, and walk in complete freedom from sin's power.

Just as we all continue to sin, we also continue to fall prey to the devil. There will be a day (when the Lord returns) we will experience the fullness of our victory through the transformation of our physical bodies and be totally free from temptation and falling. <span class="scripture-reference">Philippians 3:20-21</span> NASB For our citizenship is in heaven, from which also we eagerly wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ; (21) who will transform the body of our humble state into conformity with the body of His glory, by the exertion of the power that He has even to subject all things to Himself. Until that day we fight and overcome the world, the flesh, and the devil.

<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 16:20</span> NASB The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet.

Things that hinder us walking in a complete release from darkness

1) We continue to sin and give foothold to the devil.

2) There are patterns of sin and behavior from family lineage (cursing) that carry down to us and hinder us.

3) We fail to put on the whole armor of God and stand firm.

4) We fail to walk with God under His glory and protection.

Three places we are attacked

Experientially we are attacked on three fronts, the world, the flesh, and the devil. All three will lead to bondage ultimately to the demonic when we give way to sin.

1\. Our flesh (the war within)

We have a problem with the flesh which is bent towards evil, and will remain that way until it is crucified experientially. The completeness will not happen until Jesus returns (or we die) and we lose the mortal aspect of the flesh. <span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 5:16-18</span> NASB But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not carry out the desire of the flesh. (17) For the flesh sets its desire against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these are in opposition to one another, so that you may not do the things that you please. (18) But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the Law.

2\. The world’s system (the war outside)

The world’s system is under the control of the devil.

Joh 16:11 and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged. <span class="scripture-reference">1 John 2:15-17</span> NASB Do not love the world nor the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the

love of the Father is not in him. (16) For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh and the lust of the eyes and the boastful pride of life, is not from the Father, but is from the world. (17) The world is passing away, and also its lusts; but the one who does the will of God lives forever.

3\. The organized demonic realm (Satan, demons, principalities, and powers)

Satan oversees a vast kingdom that includes the demonic as well as a world that cooperates with them. <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 2:1-3</span> NASB And you were dead in your trespasses and sins, (2) in which you formerly walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, of the spirit that is now working in the sons of disobedience. (3) Among them we too all formerly lived in the lusts of our flesh, indulging the desires of the flesh and of the mind, and were by nature children of wrath, even as the rest. Sin holds fallen man and all of creation in bondage through the three fronts; the world, the flesh, and the devil.

Overcoming the three fronts

When we are in Christ and walk with Him we can overcome on all three fronts

\*We overcome sin with sanctification (<span class="scripture-reference">Rom 6:6</span>).

\*We overcome the flesh with crucifixion and submission (<span class="scripture-reference">Gal 3:20</span>; Ro 12:1,2).

\*We overcome the world by not becoming entangled in it and not loving it (I Jn 2:15).

\*We overcome Satan by resistance (<span class="scripture-reference">James 4:7</span>), by the armor (Eph 6), and by deliverance (<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 10:38</span>).

We need to consider a systematic approach from the New Testament (take all the facts from the Bible about a particular subject then determine what it says about it) towards how the demonic comes into the lives of Christians. This will enable us to better know how to live and resist.

## Power Encounters

In the ministry of Jesus we see several examples in which He spoke directly to the demonic (casting them out, commanding them not to speak, telling Satan to get behind Him etc.). In power encounters we directly engage in the enemy bringing God’s power to bind, break his power, and dislodge him. Power encounters are often necessary where someone has so yielded their lives to the demonic that the demonic exercise a great amount of bondage and control in their lives.

Determining the control of the demonic in the lives of someone – A combination of the following symptoms can indicate excessive control of the demonic in someone

Confirmation from the Holy Spirit that a demon is present (discerning of spirits, word of knowledge <span class="scripture-reference">1 Corinthians 12:8-11</span>) can be a very important asset. Jesus ask the name of the evil spirits in <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 8:30</span> but there is no other cases in the NT in which this is done so be careful in making that a precedent. Most of the time when Jesus directly confronted the enemy, He told them to be quiet so they wouldn’t bring more confusion.

Encountering the demonic in other people

Each time you confront the demonic in people will probably be unique. Here are some helpful suggestions.

\*Beginning prayers, praise and worship, desiring God’s presence. Psa 36:9 NASB For with You is the fountain of life; In Your light we see light.

\*Binding and loosing type prayers

\*Prayers of protection, proclaiming the blood of Jesus etc.

\*Prayers for direction on how to proceed

\*Confessions/renunciation/reclaiming on the part of the victim

\*Commanding in the name of Jesus departures/binding etc. Mar 16:17 NASB "These signs will accompany those who have believed: in My name they will cast out demons

\*Prayer for healing Asking the Lord to heal any emotional, spiritual or physical wounds from the past that may be serving as footholds for the current oppression

\*Prayer for cutting any demonic spiritual ties This severs any sinful connections of our soul with other people, spirits or objects.

What to expect -There are a wide variety of deliverance examples in the Bible. Some went quickly, and others were more drawn out. Some "classes" of demons do seem to offer more resistance than others. This may be demonic connected to sexual sins and abuse, occult practices.

There are indications that fasting and prayer may be needed in very difficult cases.

Mat 17:19-21 NASB Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, "Why could we not drive it out?" (21) \["But this kind does not go out except by prayer and fasting."\] Note: Some believe that what “goes out” in this case is the faith, others think it is the demon. \*Do not allow fear to control you. Trust in Jesus and His authority. 2Ti 1:7 For God has not given us a spirit of timidity, but of power and love and discipline.

\*Move in faith which is the key ingredient that will release God's power in your life. Things to make sure you have faith in.

-You have authority over all evil spirits through your faith in Jesus Christ (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 10:19-20</span>)

-The word of God is living and powerful; it substantiates your claim as an heir to the kingdom of God and the authority of Christ (<span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 4:7</span>)

-You have overcome the power of the devil through Jesus Christ (<span class="scripture-reference">1 John 4:4</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">1 John 5:4-5</span>)

-There may be cases where you need more than one session to work through the deliverance. Don’t give up or get discouraged.

Other important principles to keep in mind to help build faith

God’s Kingdom is superior to Satan’s kingdom.

Every kingdom divided against itself cannot stand (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:17-18</span>)

Jesus cast demons out by the finger of God (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:20</span>).

As adopted children of God, with the Holy Spirit residing in us (<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 8:15-17</span>), we too may cast demons out by the finger of God.

A fully armed "strong man" guards his palace. When a stronger man comes and overpowers him, he takes from him all his armor in which he trusted and divides his spoils (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:21-22</span>).

In Christ, we are the stronger man that overpowers demons and takes their spoils from them (men’s souls). You must first bind the strong man before you can plunder his house. In Christ, we have authority to bind things in heaven and on earth (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 12:29</span>; 16:19; 18:18-19; <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 3:27</span>).

He who is not with Jesus is against Jesus. He who does not gather with Jesus scatters (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:23</span>).

Bible references on casting out demons

The Bible features many examples of deliverance ministry. We can glean many helpful points about deliverance from studying these examples.

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 4:33-36</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 1:25-26</span> In Capernaum, a man with a demon cried out at Jesus. Jesus rebuked it, saying, “Be quiet, and come out of him!” The demon then convulsed the man and cried out with a loud voice. It threw the man down and came out of him.

<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 1:34</span> Jesus cast out many demons; He did not allow the demons to speak, because they knew him.

<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 3:10-12</span> Jesus sternly warned the unclean spirits that they should not make his identity known

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 4:38-39</span> Jesus rebuked the fever in Peter’s mother-in-law and it left immediately

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 4:40-41</span> Jesus laid hands on many people and the demons cried out and came out of them. Jesus rebuked them and did not let them speak.

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 9:37-42</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 17:14-21</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 9:14-29</span> The demon-possessed boy is delivered: This story has many interesting features. The evil spirit was able to seize the boy, cry out in a loud voice, convulse him, bruise him and cause him to foam at the mouth. The disciples were unable to cast the demon out, but Jesus was able to do so. Jesus said, “Deaf and dumb spirit, I command you, come out of him and enter him no more!” (<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 9:25</span>). Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit and healed the child (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 9:39-42</span>). Jesus later explained to the disciples that their unbelief kept them from being able to cast the demon out. Jesus also said that type of demon would not go out except by prayer and fasting (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 17:20-21</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 9:29</span>).

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 9:49-50</span> Man casting out demons in Jesus’ name

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:14-15</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 12:22-23</span> Jesus casts out a mute demon

Luke13:10-16 Jesus looses woman from a spirit of infirmity: She had been afflicted for 18 years and was bent over permanently. Jesus called her to him and said, “Woman, you are loosed from your infirmity.” He laid his hands on her and immediately she was made straight. Jesus mentioned that Satan had bound the woman (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 13:16</span>).

<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 8:28-32</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 8:26-33</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 5:1-20</span> Gaderenes (or Gerasenes) demoniacs: These men were exceedingly fierce. They cried out at Jesus, and begged him to permit them to go into the swine. Jesus said, “Come out of the man, unclean spirit!” (<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 5:1-20</span>). He also said “Go” (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 8:32</span>). Apparently the spirits didn’t leave immediately, because Jesus then asked “What is your name?” (<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 5:1-20</span>). They told him their name was "Legion" and begged him to send them into the swine. Finally the spirits left the demoniac(s) and went into the swine.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 19:11-12</span> Exorcism via handkerchiefs that touched Paul’s body

Acts 19: 13-16 Jewish exorcists attempt deliverance without faith in Jesus: These exorcists were likely spiritists/conjurers who used spells & oaths to bind spirits (see Strong’s #1845). They invoked Jesus' name without believing in Jesus nor having a relationship with him. The demons recognized the exorcist's lack of authority, jumped on them and beat them up!

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 8:6-7</span> Philip preached in Samaria to the multitudes. They heeded his teaching. Unclean spirits, crying with a loud voice, came out of many who were possessed.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 16:16-18</span> Paul casts out spirit of divination from slave girl: The girl met Paul’s team as they went to prayer. The spirit harassed Paul for days. Paul, greatly annoyed, turned and said to the spirit, “I command you in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her.” The spirit came out that hour.
