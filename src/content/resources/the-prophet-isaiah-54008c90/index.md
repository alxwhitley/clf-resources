## Theme:

The theme of the book of Isaiah is noted through out the whole book (Salvation) . It depicts Gods wrath on Israel for forsaking him and Gods Compassion on Israel for their restoration.

Isaiah Strongly promotes God’s grace and Redemptive work.

The book of Isaiah is like a miniature for shadowing of the whole bible.

Isaiah has 66 chapters the bible has 66 books

The first 39 chapters are history and prophecy = 39 OT books

The last (39-66) are prophesying Gods grace and mercy – Life and ministry of Christ and restoration = 27 NT Books

### Chpt 1-35

Represent earlier sermons of Isaiah’ in which judgment which was pending for Judah’s sins and for the sins of the surrounding nation’s - make up the central theme

But he also begins to portrait a coming Savior and a time were the people of God will be brought into their true inheritance

### Chpt 33-39

Pain a little background of the day of Isaiah’s ministry and begin to announce the coming captivity (39:6)

### Chpt 40-66

Are Isaiah’s later sermons, which are detached from the rest of the writings telling of the days of Exile in Babylon, He begins to paint a theme of Future hope, he writes as if they are already there in Exile

## Author:

Isaiah, is the author of the whole book of Isaiah.

He has been question as to whether or not he wrote the whole book because of predictions of King Cyrus and his being mentioned by name 120 before he was born in the book of Isaiah. Many believe Isaiah could not have been this accurate. But the writing styles and theme stay consistent and are more likely to have been written by Isaiah than not.

Isaiah is a type of Christ in the Old Testament. His name means (Salvation of Jehovah) His writings are the most accurate and clear of all the prophets in his description of the coming Messiah (Jesus) and the events of his life.

Isaiah is also called the Evangelist of the Old Testament.
