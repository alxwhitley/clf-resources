## PUBLIC MINISTRY

1st - A sign to unbelievers (Pentecost - a known earthly dialect)

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 14:22</span> - Thus tongues are a sign not for believers but for unbelievers

2nd - tongues for interpretation

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Cor 14:5</span> - Now I want you all to speak in tongues, but even more to prophesy. The one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in tongues, unless someone interprets, so that the church may be built up.

3rd - Tongues for Intercession - Direction and Power
