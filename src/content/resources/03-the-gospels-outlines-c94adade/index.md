THE GOSPEL: THE LIFE OF CHRIST<br>
OLD & NEW TESTAMENT SURVEY<br>
Chronological Outline:<br>
A. The Preexistent Christ.<br>
1\. Eternally the same (<span class="scripture-reference">Heb. 13:8</span>).<br>
2\. Without natural parents (<span class="scripture-reference">Heb. 7:3</span>).<br>
3\. Everlasting (<span class="scripture-reference">Micah 5:2</span>).<br>
4\. God: The Word (<span class="scripture-reference">John 1:1</span>).<br>
5\. God: The I AM (<span class="scripture-reference">John 8:58</span>).<br>
6\. God: Glorified with the Father before world existed (<span class="scripture-reference">John 17:5</span>).<br>
B. The Christ of Prophecy.<br>
1\. His Humanity (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 3:15</span>).<br>
2\. His Tribe (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 49:10</span>).<br>
3\. His Family (<span class="scripture-reference">Isa 11:1</span>).<br>
C. The Christ of Obscurity.<br>
1\. Prenatal (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 1:18-25</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 1:26-31</span>).<br>
2\. His Infancy (Matthew / Luke).<br>
3\. His Youth (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 2:41-2</span>:52) / Silent Years.<br>
D. The Ministering Christ.<br>
1\. Year of Inauguration (introduction).<br>
2\. Year of Popularity.<br>
3\. Year of Opposition.<br>
E. The Suffering Christ.<br>
1\. His Last Months.<br>
2\. His Last Week.<br>
3\. His Last Day.<br>
F. The Risen Christ.<br>
1\. Appearances to His disciples.<br>
2\. Appearances to others.<br>
G. The Glorified Christ.<br>
1\. His continued work.<br>
2\. His Second Coming.


THE GOSPEL OF JOHN<br>
OLD & NEW TESTAMENT SURVEY<br>
Basic Outline:


I. Revelation of Christ to the World (1-4).<br>
A. The pedigree of Christ in the world (1:1-18).<br>
B. The presentation of Christ to the world (1:19-51).<br>
C. The preview of Christ by the world (2-4).<br>
1\. By the wedding guests. 4. By John’s disciples.<br>
2\. By the Jews. 5. By the Samaritans.<br>
3\. By a teacher of the Jews. 6. By the Galileans.


II. Rejection of Christ by the World (5-12).<br>
A. The principles of unbelief (5-6).<br>
B. The parties of unbelief (7).<br>
C. The progress of unbelief (8-9).<br>
D. The persistence of unbelief (10-12).


III. Revelation of Christ to His Disciples (13:1-16:24).<br>
A. The pattern for the disciples’ lives (13:1-10).<br>
B. The preparation of the disciples’ hearts (13:31-14:31).<br>
C. The prospect for the disciples’ future (15).<br>
D. The promise for the disciples’ comfort (16:1-24).


IV. Reception of Christ by His Disciples (16:25-33).<br>
A. The pronouncement of the disciples’ conviction (16:25-33).<br>
B. The prayer for the disciples’ consecration (17).<br>
C. The plight of the disciples’ courage (18-19).<br>
D. The peak of the disciples’ certainty (20).<br>
E. The perfecting of the disciples’ confidence (21).


THE GOSPEL OF MATTHEW<br>
OLD & NEW TESTAMENT SURVEY<br>
Basic Outline:


I. The Person of the King (1:1-3:12).<br>
A. Ancestry—Abraham (1:1-1:17).<br>
B. Advent (arrival) —Mary (1:18-2:23).<br>
C. Ambassador—John (3:1-12).


II. The Preparation of the King (3:13-4:11).<br>
A. Baptism (3:13-17). B. Temptation (4:1-11).


III. The Proclamation of the King (4:12-9:38).<br>
A. His message (4). B. His manifesto (5-7). C. His methods (8-9).


IV. The Propagation of the King (10:1-25:46).<br>
A. The messengers (10:1-15). F. The miracles (14:1-16:12)<br>
B. The mission (10:16-42) G. The martyrs (16)<br>
C. The message (11) H. The majesty (17:1-13)<br>
D. The menaces (12). I. The model (17-20).<br>
E. The mysteries (13) J. The Messiahship (21-25).


V. The Passion of the King (26:1-27:46).<br>
A. Eating the Passover Lamb (26:1-46).<br>
B. Rejecting the Passover Lamb (26:47-27:73).<br>
C. Sacrificing the Passover Lamb (27:24-66).


VI. The Power of the King (28:1-20).<br>
A. The King’s resurrection (vv. 1-15).<br>
B. The King’s requirements (vv. 16-20).


THE GOSPEL OF MARK<br>
OLD & NEW TESTAMENT SURVEY<br>
Basic Outline:


I. The Service of the Servant (1:1-8:26).<br>
A. His ministry (1-2).<br>
B. His message (3:1-6:29).<br>
C. His miracles (6:30-8:26).


II. The Sacrifice of the Servant (8:27-15:47).<br>
A. Foretold—coming Passion (8:27-13:37).<br>
B. Focused—crises present (14).<br>
C. Fulfilled—culmination pressed (15).


III. The Sovereignty of the Servant (16).<br>
A. In arising—resurrection (vv. 1-8).<br>
B. In appearing—reappearances (vv. 9-14).<br>
C. In ascending—reception (vv.15-20).


THE GOSPEL OF LUKE<br>
OLD & NEW TESTAMENT SURVEY<br>
Basic Outline:


I. The Manhood of the Son of Man—His Advent (1:1-4:13).<br>
A. Introduction of the Son of Man (1).<br>
B. Infancy of the Son of Man (2:1-7).<br>
C. Identification of the Son of Man (2:8-4:13).<br>
1\. Adored by angels (2:8).<br>
2\. Announced by man (3:1-20).<br>
3\. Attested by God (3:21-38).<br>
4\. Attacked by Satan (4:1-13).


II. The Ministry of the Son of Man—His Activity (4:14-23:56).<br>
A. Service in light of the cross: primary ministry in Galilee (Jewish).<br>
(4:14-9:62)<br>
B. Service on the way to the cross: passing ministry in Judea, Perea<br>
(Gentiles). (10-21)<br>
C. Sacrifice on the cross: final ministry in Jerusalem. (22-23)<br>
1\. Last Supper—commended His body (22:19).<br>
2\. Last Submission—commended His soul (22:42).<br>
3\. Last Saying—“Into your hands I commend my spirit” (23:46).


III. The Majesty of the Son of Man—His Ascension (24).<br>
A. The opened grave (vv. 1-12).<br>
B. The opened Scriptures (vv. 13-35).<br>
C. The opened understanding (vv. 36-48).<br>
D. The opened heavens (vv.49-53).
