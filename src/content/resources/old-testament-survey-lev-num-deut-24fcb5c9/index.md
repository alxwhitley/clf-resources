## Old Testament Survey - Overview of the Pentateuch

### The Pentateuch (Greek) means - 5 Rolls = Law of Moses

The Torah (Hebrew) means - Law = Law of Moses

## The Pentateuch consist of 5 books:

Genesis - The book of the Origin of Man / Patriarchs / Beginning of Israel

Exodus - The way out of Bondage

Leviticus - Worship under a Levitical order

Numbers - the 2 Census & Organization of Israel’s wilderness wonderings

Deuteronomy - 2nd Giving of the Law

### Genesis - Creation – Death of Joseph, 4004 BC – 1689 BC, 2315yrs

### Exodus - From Joseph’s death –Tabernacle, 1706 –1490 BC, 216 yrs

### Leviticus - Less than 1 year at Mt Sinai – 1490BC

### Numbers - Mt Sinai – Plains of Moab, 1490 – 1451 BC, 39 yrs wilderness

### Deuteronomy - 2 months at the Plains of Moab, 1451 BC

From the creation through the life of Joseph you see the loyalty of God to work with men by design to bring about a holy line of faithful men to set up the linage for his own son to come and redeem the seed of the women prophesied about in chapter

1\. The Creation Story – God Elohim Creates The Heavens and Earth & Man

<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 1:1</span> – 2:4, God Elohim – Gods plural, Mighty God of Transcendent power. This speaks of the Godhead as a team creating the heavens and the earth.

Men always encounter God Elohim before they ever meet Jehovah or Yahweh.

<span class="scripture-reference">Gen 2:4</span>, The Lord God (Jehovah) the self-existent one came down to make man.

He shows himself not just as the God of all power and might but the lord of love and compassion who comes down in person to create his finest creation. He reveals himself as the caretaker and means for redemption when they fell.

The story of God’s plan to have a house and family (a people) through redemption, begins almost immediately in the garden.

Eve gets deceived by the serpent and denies Christ by eating from the tree of Knowledge of good and evil. The tree of life was Christ and she and Adam chose to eat from the tree of Pride and intellect. In this act Holy Creation dies and men gets thrown out of the garden. They denied the image they where made in which was after the heart of Christ. So they denied Christ himself. God’s people were separated from him. God begins Redemption right off after the fall of man. God comes looking for man and he has hidden himself (the fruit of Sin).

God rebukes the serpent, curses man and the ground with death, and prophecies the first prophecy of man’s redemption in <span class="scripture-reference">Gen 3:15</span>. Tells the women of her future seed that would bruise the head of the Devil which was afore shadowing of Jesus.

God Kills animals to cover the lives of Adam and eve for there sin. This was the second fore shadowing of the redemptive plan of Christ. Man’s sin would be covered at the price of blood

2\. Cain & Abel (first two sons of Adam & Eve) Gen. 4

Abel was a shepherd and Cain was a farmer. Abel was the first worshiper of God after sin and offered the first Lamb sacrifice to God telling of the redemption plan of God. Abel offered Sacrifices that cost him only labor. This is a picture

God would not except worship from Cain out of works

Received Abel’s offering of blood telling of Gods Grace and provision factor alone

4\. Noah and the flood (Genesis 6-8)

Noah lived where all creation had grown to hate God except him and his family alone. God repented that he makes man and wants to destroy them and decides to create again through Noah and his sons.

Noah at 600 years old builds an ark. It took Noah 120 years to build the ark

Ark – 75’ wide, 45’ high, 450’ long. It was three stories high and could accommodate about 43000 tons of weight. The present day cruise lines can only hold about 25000 tons.

Rain floods the whole earth. God limits the days of men to 120 years.

The ark is a picture of Christ himself. That all who remain holy are shut up in him and the alone are saved.

Gen 9-10

Noah receives the first covenant promise from God in the rainbow not to destroy men by a flood again

Noah’s family and his three sons and their families begin life all over again.

Sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, Japheth. (Ham is cursed, Shem is Christ line) Japheth- becomes the European countries

5\. Tower of Babel – Gen 11

Languages and cities and races begin out of Noah’s sons. As they increase the join together in Shinar to build a capitol city and a tower to reach heaven.

God comes down and distorts the languages and scatters the peoples all over the face of the earth (so races are born).

This is Genesis 1 –11, 2000 years of mans history from approx 4004 –2100BC
