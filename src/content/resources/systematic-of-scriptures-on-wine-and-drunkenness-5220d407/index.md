Systematic of Scriptures on Wine, Strong Drink, and Drunkenness

I. Systematic statistics of Biblical Passages Concerning Wine, Strong Drink, and Drunkenness

A. Biblical Statistics

250 Passages that mention drinking wine or strong drink

48% Presented in negative light or warnings against it.<br>
30% Presented in a neutral way - Neither good or bad. Consumption was mentioned as a fact of life. They had little good drinking water and alcohol was a way to insure safe liquid.<br>
16% In positive light.<br>
.08% Is giving offerings

There are 3 to 1 warnings over the positive effects of drinking<br>
101 Negative - Drinking wine or strong drink associated with negative events or in a negative light<br>
20 Warning against - Specific warnings to stay away from wine, drinking, or drunkenness<br>
75 Neutral - Drinking wine is a normal part of life<br>
35 Positive - Wine and drinking presented for positive benefits<br>
19-Offering - Wine or strong drink given as offerings to the Lord

II. Other Issues Concerning Wine, Fermented or Non-fermented Grapes, and Strong Drink

Scriptures sometimes refer to wine in the unfermented grape juice phase.<br>
Deu 32:14 Curds of cows, and milk of the flock, With fat of lambs, And rams, the breed of Bashan, and goats, With the finest of the wheat-- And of the blood of grapes you drank wine.
<span class="scripture-reference">Num 6:1-3</span> Again the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 2 "Speak to the sons of Israel and say to them, 'When a man or woman makes a special vow, the vow of a Nazirite, to dedicate himself to the LORD, 3 he shall abstain from wine and strong drink; he shall drink no vinegar, whether made from wine or strong drink, nor shall he drink any grape juice nor eat fresh or dried grapes.
<span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 65:8</span> says "Thus saith the LORD, As the new wine is found in the cluster, and one saith, Destroy it not; for a blessing is in it: so will I do for my servants' sakes, that I may not destroy them all." (Alcoholic wine in the cluster? No, the juice of the grape while in the cluster could not be intoxicating.)
<span class="scripture-reference">Joel 1:10</span> "The field is wasted, the land mourneth; for the corn is wasted: the new wine is dried up, the oil languisheth." (This refers to grapes dried up in the fields, which could not be intoxicating.)
<span class="scripture-reference">Jeremiah 48:33</span> says "And joy and gladness is taken from the plentiful field, and from the land of Moab;
and I have caused wine to fail from the winepresses: none shall tread with shouting; their shouting shall be no shouting." (The wine of fresh squeezed grapes coming out of the winepress is grape juice and could not be fermented.) Certainly other passages could be considered, but these are sufficient to illustrate that the word "wine" can refer to alcoholic wine or simply grape juice.

Wine is seen as the products of grapes "new wine" is more of a non fermented.<br>
Deu 12:17 "You are not allowed to eat within your gates the tithe of your grain or new wine or oil, or the firstborn of your herd or flock, or any of your votive offerings which you vow, or your freewill offerings, or the contribution of your hand.<br>
Deu 18:4 "You shall give him the first fruits of your grain, your new wine, and your oil, and the first shearing of your sheep.

"New wine" can be construed to occasionally referred to as fermented wine but these occurrences can be questioned.<br>
Jdg 9:13 "But the vine said to them, 'Shall I leave my new wine, which cheers God and men, and go to wave over the trees?'
<span class="scripture-reference">Hos 4:11</span> Harlotry, wine and new wine take away the understanding.
