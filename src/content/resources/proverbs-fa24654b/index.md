## Old Testament Survey- Proverbs

## The Theme of Proverbs:

The book of Proverbs is a collection of short statements setting forth moral lessons of life.

It is a practical book applying the principles of righteousness, purity and godliness to everyday Life

It teaches not wisdom based on human understanding but wisdom based on the fear of the lord.

The grand theme is wisdom based upon righteous character.

## Author:

Solomon who wrote the first 24 chapters and 2 prophets Agur & Lemuel (who wrote the last two Chpts)
