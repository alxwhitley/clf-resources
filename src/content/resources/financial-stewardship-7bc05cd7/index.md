## Slide 11

Tithe<br>
WHAT IS A “TITHE”?<br>
“Tithe” comes from a Hebrew word which means tenth. A tithe of one’s income (or increase); therefore, is one-tenth (10%). Tithing refers to the practice of systematically giving 10% of one’s income back to the Lord for His work.<br>
Tithing was the common practice of holy men of God in Old Testament days.<br>
  Prior to the giving of the law, God’s people tithed voluntarily.<br>
    Abraham gave a tenth to Melchizedek (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 14:18-20</span>)<br>
    Jacob gave a tenth at Bethel (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 28:10-22</span>)<br>
  When God gave the law to Moses, tithing was a major part of His overall financial system (<span class="scripture-reference">Leviticus 27:30-33</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Numbers 18:20-32</span>)<br>
  Jesus confirmed tithing in His teaching and practice (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 23:23</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:42</span>; 18:12; <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 7:1-21</span>)
<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:42</span> “You Pharisees are hypocrites! For you are obsessed with peripheral issues, like paying meticulous tithes on the smallest herbs that grow in your gardens.\[a\] Of course, these matters you should do, but when you unjustly cheat others, you ignore the most important duty of all: to walk in the love of God.\[b\] Readjust your values and place first things first.
