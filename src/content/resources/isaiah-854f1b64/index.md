## Theme:

The theme of the book of Isaiah is noted through out the whole book. It depicts Gods wrath on Israel for forsaking him and Gods Compassion on Israel for their restoration.

Isaiah Strongly promotes God’s grace and Redemptive work.

The book of Isaiah is like a miniature for shadowing of the whole bible.

Isaiah has 66 chapters the bible has 66 books

The first 39 chapters are history and prophecy = 39 OT books

The last (39-66) are prophesying Gods grace and mercy – Life and ministry of Christ and restoration = 27 NT Books

## Author:

Isaiah is the author of the book of Isaiah. He has been question as to whether or not he wrote the whole book because of predictions of King Cyrus and his being mentioned by name 120 before he was born in the book of Isaiah. Many believe Isaiah could not have been this accurate. But the writing styles and theme stay consistent and are more likely to have been written by Isaiah than not.

Isaiah is a type of Christ in the Old Testament. His name means (Salvation of Jehovah)

His writings are the most accurate and clear of all the prophets in his description of the coming Messiah (Jesus) and the events of his life.

Isaiah is also called the Evangelist of the Old Testament.

Time Frame covered in Isaiah = 62 years

This conclusion is made because of Isaiah’s high understanding of the political affairs of his day and his knowledge of world affairs. And because of the depth of his writings.
