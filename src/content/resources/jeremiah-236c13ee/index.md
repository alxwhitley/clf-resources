## Jeremiah – The Weeping Prophet -

Read the Book He who wept!

Name means Established of The Lord

Jeremiah is Known as being the (Major Prophet) of Judah’s Darkest Hour

We know more about the personality of Jeremiah than any other Prophet in the bible.

Jeremiah was the son of Hilkiah the Priest (Same priest that found the law for King Josiah and raised King Josiah)

<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 1:1-3</span>

The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,

Jeremiah was about 20 years old when he began his prophetic ministry, which he had received at a younger age. ( Jer 1)

<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 1:4-9</span>

Now the word of the LORD came to me saying,

5 "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, And before you were born I consecrated you; I have appointed you a prophet to the nations." 6 Then I said, "Alas, Lord GOD! Behold, I do not know how to speak, Because I am a youth." 7 But the LORD said to me, "Do not say, "I am a youth,' Because everywhere I send you, you shall go, And all that I command you, you shall speak. 8 "Do not be afraid of them, For I am with you to deliver you," declares the LORD.

9 Then the LORD stretched out His hand and touched my mouth, and the LORD said to me, "Behold, I have put My words in your mouth.

Jeremiah (Like Isaiah) belonged to the upper class in Jerusalem. Because of this he was able to demand the respect of Kings and dignitaries and men of official rank. (We know this because he was able to buy land) and he prophesied openly before the kings many of times.

Time frame of His Ministry

Jeremiah Prophesied in Judah between (40-50 years). Trying to win Judah back to repentance.

Jeremiah Prophesied during 5 kings Reigns of Judah

18 yrs under Josiah and most of the rest under King Jekoiakin , King Zedekiah

<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 1:1-3</span>

The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin,

2 to whom the word of the LORD came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign. 3 It came also in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah, king of Judah, until the exile of Jerusalem in the fifth month.

King Josiah is 8 years old son becomes king. (Josiah raised by a priest Hilkiah + prophetess + Shaphan the Scribe)

Destroys the alters of false worship built by Manasseh and reads the law in Israel.

Restores worship back to the temple and Israel – Help of prophets Jeremiah

King Jehoahaz – Josiah’s son reigns for three months taken captive by Egypt for rebelling

King Jehoiakim- Josiah’s son reigns 11 years in Judah

Known for the evil he committed- killed the prophet Uriah for prophesying against his evil.

King Jekoiakin – Johoiakim’s son ruled in his place. Gets plundered in 2 captivity of Judah and is taken to Babylon.

King Zedekiah – Josiah’s Son Rules in his place. He also rebels against Babylon and the city is destroyed by King Nebuchadnezzar and Zedekiah is imprisoned for life

Portrait of Jeremiah’s Life & The Three Captivities of Israel by Babylon.

Jeremiah was known as the weeping Prophet because of his broken heart and Godly sorrow he revealed over the nation of Judah and his own life. His heart and heaviness can be heard in his prophecies.

"Why have they provoked Me with their graven images, with foreign idols?" 20 "Harvest is past, summer is ended, And we are not saved." 21 For the brokenness of the daughter of my people I am broken; I mourn, dismay has taken hold of me. 22 Is there no balm in Gilead? Is there no physician there? Why then has not the health of the daughter of my people been restored?

All my trusted friends, Watching for my fall, say: "Perhaps he will be deceived, so that we may prevail against him And take our revenge on him."

Jeremiah is pressed by Gods Anger and Sorrow and the loss of his own life. He was not allowed to marry because of Israel’s pending doom.

Jeremiah witnessed the first captivity under Jehoiakim’s reign fulfilling his prophesies against the king and saw the Prophet Daniel carried off to Babylon.

1st Captivity - In

606 BC The Babylonians defeated the Assyrians and The Egyptians. They immediately claimed all of their territory including Judah under King Jehoiakim’s Reign.

Nebuchadnezzar went straight to Jerusalem and forced the loyalty of Judah

To assure the Loyalty he took favored young men to Babylon including The Prophet Daniel

2nd Captivity-

Then Jehoiakin took reign and Jeremiah Then saw the 2nd Captivity by Babylon in which this king was killed and the Prophet Ezekiel was taken to Babylon.

3 Years later Jehoiakim rebelled against Babylon and refused to pay loyalty so Nebuchadnezzar hired certain bands of raiders from the ammonites, Moabites, Syrians, and Chaldeans to plunder Jerusalem.

<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 25:1-9</span>

The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah (that was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon),

2 which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,

3 "From the thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah, even to this day, these twenty-three years the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened.

4 "And the LORD has sent to you all His servants the prophets again and again, but you have not listened nor inclined your ear to hear, 5 saying, "Turn now everyone from his evil way and from the evil of your deeds, and dwell on the land which the LORD has given to you and your forefathers forever and ever; 6 and do not go after other gods to serve them and to worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands, and I will do you no harm.'

7 "Yet you have not listened to Me," declares the LORD, "in order that you might provoke Me to anger with the work of your hands to your own harm. 8 "Therefore thus says the LORD of hosts, "Because you have not obeyed My words, 9 behold, I will send and take all the families of the north,' declares the LORD, "and I will send to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, My servant, and will bring them against this land and against its inhabitants and against all these nations round about; and I will utterly destroy them and make them a horror and a hissing, and an everlasting desolation.

Finally Jehoiakim dies and his son becomes king (Jehoiakin)

Three months into Jehoiakin’s reign Nebuchadnezzar attacks Jerusalem and Takes Jehoiakin and his family and 10,000 leading citizens to Babylon leaving mostly poor in Jerusalem.

The Prophet Ezekiel was among these men taken in the 2nd Captivity.

3rd Captivity – Jerusalem Destroyed –

Then Zedekiah took reign and Jeremiah again warned him to repent and live Holy before God. He refused.

Zedekiah Josiah’s son is made ruler in Judah Reigned 11 years

The prophet Jeremiah was the main prophetic voice of the day but Zedekiah

For I have spoken the word," declares the LORD. 6 Then Jeremiah the prophet spoke all these words to Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem

Zedekiah also rebelled against the Babylonians thinking Egypt would help. Nebuchadnezzar immediately attacked the city and starved the city for 3 years until they surrendered

Zedekiah and his army ran and left the city unprotected but was caught in the plains of Jericho/He and his sons were taken to Babylon and his sons were killed in front of him and then he was blinded and left to die in prison.

Nebuchadnezzar destroyed Jerusalem and plundered the temple and palace of the kings. He burned the city with fire as well.

He took 25,000 people to Babylon and left the poorest people in Judah.

<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 39:9-10</span>

9 As for the rest of the people who were left in the city, the deserters who had gone over to him and the rest of the people who remained, Nebuzaradan the captain of the bodyguard carried them into exile in Babylon. 10 But some of the poorest people who had nothing, Nebuzaradan the captain of the bodyguard left behind in the land of Judah, and gave them vineyards and fields at that time.

The prophet Jeremiah was able to stay by choice in Jerusalem then was carried to Egypt were he died.

Jerusalem was destroyed and remained that way for the next ½ century Jeremiah witnessed the destruction of Jerusalem and saw all of it’s noble taken to Babylon. Jeremiah remained in Jerusalem with the poor and prophesied to those who had gone into captivity. (The book of lamentations)

Judah at this point ceases to exist under Babylonian rule.

Jeremiahs Message after Captivity

Was directed at the scattered Jews in Palestine, Egypt, and those captive in Babylon.

Jeremiah messages were two part

Warnings of Judah’s Future and Captivity

Second half - messages of Hope to scattered & Captive Jews of restoration

Jeremiah like Ezekiel began to preach and pronounce personal responsibility for individual sin. The Exile period produced a spiritual reformation for the Jews.
