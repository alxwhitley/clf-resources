SMART GOALS<br>
Concrete goals are your wellness milestones.<br>
Let's set goals that are SMART:


Specific: Clear and concise.

S<br>
Measurable: Quantifiable to track progress.

M<br>
Achievable: Attainable to remain motivating.

A<br>
Relevant: Aligned with your larger wellness vision.

R<br>
Time-bound: Encased within a timeframe.

T

Sketch your goals with kindness, remembering that they are fluid and can adapt to your journey’s needs.
