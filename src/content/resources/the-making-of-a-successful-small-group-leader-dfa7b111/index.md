## Slide 3

A. PRAYERFUL LEADER<br>
You can measure your dependence on God by the amount of time you spend in prayer. The leader should be consistent:<br>
1\. In personal prayer (<span class="scripture-reference">I Tim. 2:8</span>).<br>
2\. In praying for those over him in the Lord (<span class="scripture-reference">I Tim. 2:2</span>).<br>
3\. In praying for those for whom he is responsible for (<span class="scripture-reference">Phil. 1:4, 9</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Col. 1:3, 9</span>; Phm.<br>
4\. In praying for specific needs that arise among your people (Jam. 5:13-16).<br>
11/18/2025<br>

## Slide 4

B. DILIGENT/HARDWORKING LEADER<br>
This refers to a leader who is constant and steady in applying him or herself to the task, is persevering, persistent, industrious and not idle or negligent.<br>
He who has a slack hand becomes poor, but the hand of diligent makes rich. <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 10:4</span><br>
Be diligent to know the state of your flocks, and attend to your herds <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 27:23</span><br>
11/18/2025<br>
4

## Slide 5

C. CONFIDENTIAL LEADER (<span class="scripture-reference">Ecclesiastes 3:7</span>)<br>
A time to keep silence, and a time to speak…<br>
1\. The word "confidential" literally means "with faith." When people come to you "with faith" you must not let them down.<br>
a. Confident. When people come to you they want to have the confidence that you will protect them and their interests.<br>
b. Confidence. If people are going to have confidence in leaders, they must feel that leaders can be trusted.<br>
11/18/2025<br>

## Slide 6

3\. The confidential leader practices several LAWS<br>
The LAW OF LOVE—Always seeking the best interest of the other person.<br>
Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all sins. <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 10:12</span><br>
He who covers a transgression seeks love, but he who repeats a matter separates friends.(<span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 17:9</span>)<br>
11/18/2025<br>
6

## Slide 7

The LAW OF TRUST<br>
4\. The confidential leader practices the LAW OF TRUST—Always remaining loyal to other members of the body of Christ.
<span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 11:13</span>
13 A gossip betrays a confidence,<br>
but a trustworthy person keeps a secret.<br>
11/18/2025<br>

## Slide 8

The LAW OF EDIFICATION<br>
5\. The confidential leader practices the LAW OF EDIFICATION—Making sure that he only speaks that which builds up and strengthens.<br>
“Let no corrupt word proceed out of your mouth, but what is good for necessary edification, that it may impart grace to the hearers. And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.” <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:29-30</span><br>
11/18/2025<br>

D. GRACIOUS LEADER
<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 4:6</span> ESV

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:29</span> ESV

## Slide 10

E. FACILITATOR<br>
As a facilitator of the Small Group , you are in the position of a leader to:<br>
1\. Make it easier for others to find meaningful relationships.<br>
2\. Make it easier for all the needs of the group to be met.<br>
3\. Make it easier for people to find their place in the body.<br>
11/18/2025<br>

## Slide 11

ADDITIONAL TIPS FOR SUCCESS AS A SMALL GROUP LEADER<br>
1\. Have confidence in God's choice (<span class="scripture-reference">John 15:16</span>).<br>
a. Of you as a leader (<span class="scripture-reference">Eph. 2:10</span>).<br>
b. Of the people he has placed in your care.<br>
2\. Share the vision of the leadership.<br>
a. Make it your own.<br>
b. Speak it often to the people; let them sense your personal enthusiasm.<br>
11/18/2025<br>

## Slide 12

3\. Have a goal in everything.<br>
a. Don’t have activities simply for the sake of activities. Focus activities on areas consistent with the purpose of the cell group.<br>
• Building Relationships<br>
• Deepening Maturity<br>
• Equipping for Service<br>
• Catching a Vision for their Life<br>
• Giving Opportunity for Service<br>

## Slide 15

5\. See yourself as an extension of the eldership of the local church<br>
Teach only what you have heard from your leaders.<br>
 Don’t teach from tapes and materials that are inconsistent with what you have heard the leadership say…<br>
b. Observe the standards for leadership in your local church. In the following areas:<br>
 Alcohol Consumption, Entertainment – What is the leadership standard in your local church?<br>
c. Minister out of a gracious spirit.<br>
