## Questions and Answers:

Q: How do you release judgment prophetically with love?

A: The prophetic is called to “hire and fire”. Wisdom must be sued to determine if a public or private word is appropriate.

Be mindful that public release of a word of judgment can have a serious and pervasive impact upon many people.

Q: Some people have been abused by the prophetic—how do you restore people or help people if they are in a church without structure?

A: Be led by the Spirit on how explicitly to operate; for example in a Baptist church context, when called upon to pray for a group of youth leaders once, I did not blatantly prophesy over them everything that I saw, but prayed that God would respond to specific/personal situations that He was showing me.

Q: Growth—how does one grow in the prophetic; especially if it can be recognized that some functions are beyond your “grade level” and/or if one is alone, in a desert place?

A: Seek out a company of prophets to teach and to guide you with the release of words; know that one of the goals for people in Nairobi is to bring other prophets onsite to activate prophetic people there and pull them into ministry moments; access relational prophetic voices.

Q: How does the prophetic function in worship ministry? How should it be managed/facilitated since worship is to the whole church body?

A: Administration is important. Relationships are important amongst leaders who can learn to communicate with one another for adjustments and course corrections within a meeting or atmosphere.

Walk with covering, submission, and practice.

Listen for the “audibles” outside of natural administration and submit it to the team.

Maintain a balance of spontaneity and prepared order.
