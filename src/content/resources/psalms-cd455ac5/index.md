## Old Testament Survey- Psalms

## Theme:

The book is a collection of Hebrew Poetry and Music setting forth the worship and describing the spiritual experiences of the Jewish people.

In the Historical books we see

God speaking about man

In the Prophetic books we see

Speaking to man

In the Psalms we see

Man speaking to God- pouring forth his heart in prayer and praise & speaking about God, describing and exalting him for the manifestations of his attributes.

## The Grand theme is:

Man is able to Praise God in his life in all circumstances, for his faithfulness in the past is a guarantee of his promised faithfulness in the future.
