## Prophetic Ministry

There are 3 levels of Prophetic Ministry

Personal Prophets: Edification & Exhortation I Cor 14

Building Prophets: Haggai, Zechariah

Prophesied to the building process and labored to build

Kingdom Prophets: Watchmen over nations

## The Role of Seasoned prophets:

Called to confront what’s in error in the church and things that would prevent God’s people from growing; They confront & build, commends for what is good and what is lacking.
