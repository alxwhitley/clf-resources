We are now going to tackle the area most credit repair companies ignore.<br>
Credit Utilization counts 30% towards your credit score. This means credit utilization is almost as important as payment history.<br>
Credit utilization is the ratio of your outstanding credit card balances to your credit card limits. It measures the amount of credit limit you are using. For example, if your balance is $300 and your credit limit is $1,000, then your credit utilization for that credit card is 30 percent.<br>
The Smart Credit System utilizes a concept called Income Shifting to address credit utilization.<br>
The only way to fix credit utilization is to pay down your debts or increase your credit limits. If you have bad credit, increasing credit limits will not be an option.<br>
Most people are living paycheck to paycheck and have no breathing room to rapidly attack their debt. In fact, they are behind on debts and cannot find a way to catch up.<br>
The Smart Credit System starts off with the first strategy of correcting tax withholdings. According to the IRS, 80% of taxpayers have their tax withholding form filled out incorrectly. With the average refund being $3100, this strategy could add an extra $255 dollars per month back into a person's household.<br>

The next strategy inside of the income shifting concept is creating business income. The reason most people have bad credit is because they have limited income which means they are short each month being able to pay all their bills on time.<br>
