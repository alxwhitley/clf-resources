## Slide 2

The twelve Historical Books chronologically follow the Law but are grouped together because of their common purpose.<br>
They are commonly separated into three sections:<br>
Pre–Kingdom Era.<br>
Joshua, Judges and Ruth were written before the Israelites insisted and gaining a king.<br>
Kingdom Era<br>
These six books were written during the united kingdom period as well as the divided kingdom period. The later period was split into the south (Judah) and the north (Israel). Each had its own king.<br>
Post–Kingdom Era<br>
Ezra, Nehemiah and Esther were written during this period that led God's people back into the land. They had no king during this time.<br>
The five Poetical Books were mostly written during the time of 2 Samuel by Kings David and Solomon. The book of Job was written during the period of Genesis

## Slide 3

12 Historical Books<br>
Joshua – the Conquest of Canaan<br>
Judges – The Period of the 15 Judges<br>
Ruth - Story of Naomi & Ruth and Christ our Redeemer<br>
I Samuel - Birth of Samuel (Israel’s Last Judge) to Death of King Saul<br>
2 Samuel - Covers the 3 Anointing of King David and his Reign as King<br>
1 Kings - Death of David to the reign of Jehoram over Israel (118) years<br>
2 Kings - Jehoram over Judah to Ahaziah over Israel to the captivity period (308 years)<br>
I Chronicles - 1 & 2 Chronicles deals with the same time frame as Samuel & Kings<br>
2 Chronicles from death of Saul to decree of Cyrus (520 years)<br>
Ezra - The return of the Jews from captivity in Babylon and the rebuilding of the temple<br>
Nehemiah - The return of the Jews from captivity in Babylon, the rebuilding of the Jerusalem<br>
Esther – God's protection of Israel

## Slide 4

Poetry and Wisdom Books<br>
All of the Poetry Books and Prophetic Books fit somewhere into the history represented in the records from Genesis – Nehemiah.<br>
Job – aspiration for mediation by Christ<br>
Psalms- aspiration for communion with Christ<br>
Proverbs- aspiration for wisdom in Christ<br>
Ecclesiastes- aspiration for ultimate satisfaction<br>
Song of Solomon- aspiration for union in love with Christ
