## Old Testament Survey – Exodus

### Exodus – covers the time of Israel leaving Egypt 1706 – 1490 BC

Jacob’s family lived in the land of Egypt from about 2100 BC – 1580 BC under the rule of friendly Kings known as the Hyksos or Shepherd Kings. In about 1580 the Egyptian’s expelled the Hyksos’s and set in a new line of Kings known as Ahmose or ahmosis 1 ( the 18th dynasty of Egypt) who new not Joseph and his loyalty to Egypt.

They began to fear the success and size of Israel in the land. Began to set taskmasters over Israel and enslave them. (thus, Abraham’s prophetic word comes to pass) and they are enslaved for 400 years

1\. Miraculous deliverance of Israel Exodus 1-13:16

Taken in by Pharaoh’s daughter and nursed by own mother.

Hears of Hebrew customs and beliefs from mother and learns the education of Egypt in pharaoh’s house.

Gets a heart for the Hebrews and Kills an Egyptian. At age 40

Flees to Midian and marries and shepherd sheep learning the heart of a pastor until 80 years old

Has burning bush experience at Mt. Horeb (the place of death)

Returns to Aaron / Elders in Egypt

Goes to Pharaoh / 10 plagues
