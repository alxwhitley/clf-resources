The 5-Fold Offices (Pg. 19-22)

Apostles, Prophets, Evangelist, Pastors and Teachers (<span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:11-13</span>) – Pg. 19

Primary Goal is to equip the saints and bring the church to maturity

All 5 offices are still in operation today, because fullness has not yet been obtained

Every Elder is a five-fold calling, not all five-fold callings are local church elders

Leaders are only called to one office, but may do the work of another office through Grace provided (Example: Prophet with apostolic wisdom, Prophet with a teaching grace)

## Establishes Churches and Leadership Teams

Strengthens existing churches

Helps formulate doctrine

Father among leaders and churches

Prophet

God’s Mouthpiece revealing the revelation of Christ (<span class="scripture-reference">Eph 3:5</span>) – (Pg. 20)

Works alongside apostles to lay foundations in the church and establish leadership

Receives messages and direction from God to help:

Direct, Warn, Inform, Encourage, Exhort, Edify, Console, Affirm, Appoint

Sometimes foretells future events

Identifies grace and calling on future leaders to establish them

Evangelist

One who proclaims the Gospel of Jesus Christ for the purpose of winning new souls

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 21:8</span> (Pg. 21)

Phillip preaches to masses to the lone eunuch

Not a one man show, but called to train and equip the body with the spirit of evangelism

Pastor

One who feeds and cares for the sheep and body of Christ

<span class="scripture-reference">Ezekiel 34:12-16</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">John 10:10-15</span> (Pg. 21)

Is not the guy who runs the church. Typical models are incorrect.

One-man leadership forces a single man to operate as all 5 offices. (not God’s design).

Pastor is a member of the leadership team

Heart for the sheep like the evangelist has for the lost.

Teacher

One who is skilled and anointed to unfold the revelations hidden in the Word of God

Jn 20:16 (Pg. 22)

Jesus is all in all (Pg. 22)

Jesus is THE Apostle, Prophet, Evangelist, Pastor and Teacher. Offices carry a part of Christ himself.

He appoints offices to build his people and church

We are to recognize those God has called and appointed over us and respond to that calling and office

Other Gifts to the Church

Psalmist, helps, administrations, hospitality, giving, intercession ect.

People can and do operate in submitted graces even when not in direct leadership, but under leadership

The church belongs to God: It is his Government, His gifts, His callings, His leaders, His People
