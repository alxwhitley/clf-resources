## How a WOUND BECOMES INFECTED:

In Mt 12 Jesus refers to the devil as Beelzebub, which means ‘Lord of the Flies’

Open wounds attracts flies. Flies induce infection. Infection untreated can lead to death

Adult flies are attracted to the smell of open sores, pus, decaying flesh as a signal of food source.

A female fly and lay eggs 100’s of eggs directly on or near the injured skin

which hatch w/in 24 hrs into maggots (worm-like baby flies).

The maggots use tiny mouthparts to feed on the tissue.

While some fly species only eat dead, decaying flesh, more dangerous species actively burrow deep into healthy living tissue, muscle, and bone
