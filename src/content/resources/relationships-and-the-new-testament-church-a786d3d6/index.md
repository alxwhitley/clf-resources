## CHURCH LIFE CLASSS

## RELATIONSHIPS AND THE NEW TESTAMENT CHURCH

PREFACE: The picture we have from Scripture of the New Testament Church shows us that "Church"

was a relational way of life. Church wasn't just a meeting, or a building, but the people of

God walking out their common life together in Christ. Church, like salvation, was truly a way

of life.

## I. EIGHT MODELS WE SEE IN THE CONTEMPORARY CHURCH SCENE

A. The Lecture Hall- A place where believers go to hear the Bible expounded. Some go so far as

to name their church organization "The Teaching Center."

B. The Theater - A place where faithful gather to witness the drama of the sacrament and other traditions enacted before them.

C. The Corporation Church - A place where an efficient and highly program-oriented organization

a full-time pastoral team involved in retailing religion to the masses.

D. The Country Club Church - A place where people join to have certain needs met, just as one might

join any other organization to have other needs met.

E. The Mystical Any Meeting Church - Any meeting where the faithful of the Mystical Body Of Christ may gather. In this type of gathering no organization, leadership, or practical commitment is necessary because we are all "one in the Spirit."

The Event/Revival Center Church – The Church is centered around getting people to come to

events or meetings. Success is measured in the number of people gathered in buildings.

G. The Personality Church – The Church is centered around a Charismatic leader with great gifts

and ability. As a one man leader, if he falls or leaves the church will scatter due to it being built

around one person.

H. The Issue Church – The Church is built on issues. The issue is the thing that draws and keeps the

church together. As long as the issue is to the forefront the people have a common rally point. Issues

can be anything from Politics, Home Schooling, Abortion, etc.

I. In light of these various ideas present when it comes to Church we need to look at the picture of what the Church looked like in the New Testament and build after that pattern.

## II. THE EXPERIENCE OF THE EARLY CHURCH

This was the Church that "turned the world upside down". <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 17:6</span>. They made a great impact in the

earth. This is in great contrast to society’s view of the Church today as boring, irrelevant, impersonal,

and full of hypocrites.

## Quote from "Restoration In The Church"

A. The Church of Pentecost.

<span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:36-47</span>; 4:31-33; 5:42

Authentic Christianity "Coming under the loving Lordship of Jesus, and being joined to a company

of imperfect people who are learning to live a new life in a new way."

B. One of the hallmarks was that they were devoted to fellowship. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:42</span>.

Fellowship - Gk Koinonia - a having in common, partnership, a shared life, or a common life. It is

where we get our word “community”.

Fellowship/community was their \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.

Their view of Church was not a building, a meeting, or an organization. They saw it as a joined

together group of people (people in relationship with God and each other) relating and working

together on a regular basis to extend the Kingdom of God.

A "Sunday Morning Christian" was no more in their way of thinking than a "Sunday Morning Church". They were radically devoted to God and to one another.

Biblical Fellowship was more than a two or three times a week meeting for five minutes of "fellowship." It was a lifestyle in which they shared a common life together.

B. Examples of the life of fellowship in the Early Church's experience.

1\. They prayed, broke bread, and received teaching together. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:43</span>

2\. They were together and held things in common. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:44</span>

3\. They shared material possessions as any had need. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:45, 4</span>:32

4\. They walked together living their lives on a daily basis. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:46</span>

5\. They ate meals together in their homes. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:46</span>

6\. They praised God together. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:47, 4</span>:24

7\. They met in the Temple and from house to house. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:46</span>

8\. They were united in purpose, one heart one soul. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 4:32</span> (They had a common destiny in

God together).

9\. There wasn't a needy one among them due to their benevolence. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 4:32</span>

## III. RELATIONSHIPS IN THE NEW TESTAMENT

A. The New Testament was written to Local Churches (groups of believers in geographic areas who were joined together in relationships) and it is only in the context of Local Churches that it can be understood.

Billy Graham in book "Approaching Hoof beats" - "He (the apostle John, in Revelation) wrote to

individual Churches, small clusters of believers, leaders and followers together. At the heart of

these letters is God's assumption that we belong together at work, and at worship in a Local

Church. I am convinced that the cluster of believers of which you are a part, those brothers and

sisters in Christ with whom you join to pray, study, give, and witness, is the basic unit through

which God is working to redeem the world."

B. The New Testament gives us very clear guidelines on how we are to walk in relationships

together as a Local Church.

1\. Be devoted to and prefer one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 2:10</span>

2\. Be of the same mind and live in harmony. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 12:16</span>

3\. Love one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 13:8</span>

4\. Don't judge one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 14:13</span>

5\. Pursue harmony and build up one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 14:19</span>

6\. Be like-minded and sympathetic to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:5</span>

7\. Accept one another, like Christ accepted you. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:7</span>

8\. Admonish, council or instruct one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:14</span>

9\. Care for one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Cor 12:25</span>

10\. Serve one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 5:13</span>

11\. Bear each other's burdens. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 6:2</span>

12\. Bear with each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:2</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:12</span>

13\. Be kind and helpful to one another, and forgive each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:32</span>

14\. Be accountable to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 5:21</span>

15\. Be honest, don't lie to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:9</span>

16\. Teach and admonish one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:16</span>

17\. Increase in love for one another. 1Thes 3:12

18 .Comfort and cheer up one another. 1Thes 4:18

19\. Exhort and encourage each other daily. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 3:13</span>

20\. Stimulate and provoke others to love and good works. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 19:24</span>

21\. Don't speak evil about each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 4:11</span>

22\. Don't complain about each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 5:9</span>

23\. Love one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 1:22</span>; 1Jn 3:11; 3:23; 4:7; 4:11; 4:12; 2Jn 5

24\. Be hospitable towards one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 4:9</span>

25\. Be submitted to and humble towards one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 5:5</span>

It is obvious that in order to walk in these instructions for fellowship it will take more than just a

corporate meeting one or two times a week. It is to be a way of life.

C. At the heart of the purpose of God for Local Churches is the need to walk together in right

relationships.

There is a need for unity among the Local Churches in order to accurately grow and express the

Kingdom of God.
