2\. He teaches us.

3\. He convicts us.
<span class="scripture-reference">John 16:8-11</span>
And when he comes, he will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment: 9 concerning sin, because they do not believe in me; 10 concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father, and you will see me no longer; 11 concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world is judged.<br>
\- We don’t know what is holy, right, and pure without the Holy Spirit. He convicts us when we sin.<br>
4\. He guides us.
<span class="scripture-reference">John 16:13</span>

\- A few things here<br>
\- The Holy Spirit is the Spirit of truth<br>
\- He hears directly from God and only speaks what God speaks<br>
\- Where does he lead us? He leads us into truth. Remember we don’t know sin unless the Holy Spirit convicts us. We don’t know where to go unless the Holy Spirit leads us.<br>
\- Remember in Acts 19 that when Paul laid hands on the men in Ephesus, they began to prophesy. So did the elders in Numbers when the Holy Spirit came upon them. The Holy Spirit can declare to us things that are to come<br>
(prophecy) because He is the Spirit of Truth. <span class="scripture-reference">John 16:13</span> tells us that He will declare to us the things to come because He stands outside of time and knows the beginning from the end.<br>
