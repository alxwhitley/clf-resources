## Old Testament Prophets – Jonah - (The Pharisee Prophet)

The book of Jonah is a book that is very unique in nature. It covers not the life of Jonah or the direct messages of Jonah, but merely a small portion of his life. The book of Jonah is like a novel skillfully pinned allowing us to look into the life of society, the heart and mind of the Prophet, and the response of the gentile world to the message of a merciful and sovereign God.

The Whale Issue: Could Jonah have really been eaten by a whale & lived?

Frank Bullen’s – “Cruise of the Cachalot” displays some idea of the size of the whales of the deep – The Sperm Whale in particular. Frank is an experienced whaler and records events he has actually seen. He records of numerous times where whales over 70 feet long were caught and killed. The size & weight were estimated at 15 tons. He records the events of one trip of how a 15-foot shark was found whole in the belly of a whale. The one piece of interesting evidence to the Jonah story is that when a sperm whale is dieing he records, the whale actually ejects the contents of its stomach. He records one such event in his book, were a whale ejected its stomach contents in a pile as large as the ships Hatch house. 8 feet x 6 feet x 6 feet

The James Bartley story- James Bartley was a British seaman aboard the whaler ship star of the East. In 1891 when out at sea Mr. Bartley was part of an attempt to capture a sperm whale off the coast of Labrador. The whale upset one of the boats and the men were thrown into the sea. All were saved but two. These men were thought to have drowned. The Star of East managed to capture and kill he sperm whale and towed it to shore. 2 ½ days later they opened the whales belly and to their amazement found one of their sea comrades Mr. James Bartley. He was unconscious but still alive. He suffered intensely afterwards but finally made a complete recovery. The story was fully investigated by the leading journalist of the day M de Parville, editor of the journal des Debats. Who conclude all the recorded events and statements from Captain and crew all coincided and was worthy of belief.
