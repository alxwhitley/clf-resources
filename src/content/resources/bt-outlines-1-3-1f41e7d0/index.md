Provision

Salvation is God’s idea – His gracious initiation towards man, to restore broken relationship and restore us to the state we were in the Garden with Him.

Salvation includes being saved from sin, healed from sickness and delivered from the power of the devil. The Greek word Sozo reflects this (saved, healed, delivered)

Salvation goes well beyond just getting to heaven – God creates a new nature inside of us, made in His image, when He saves us and comes to live in us. As we grow, we learn to live from this new nature.

We receive the gift of salvation through repentance and receiving God’s forgiveness. We make a public declaration through baptism.
