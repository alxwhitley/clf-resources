## Old Testament Prophets – Scott Woodard

## Object of Class:

The Student will complete this class with a basic understanding of the OT Prophets

They will study the Life, Culture, purpose, and calling of the 3 Major and 12 Minor Prophets – and a light overview of Daniel

## The Class Overview will cover:

The Prophets themselves (the calling, Kingdom of Ministry, time frame, Purpose)

Will cover what the prophet wrote (which book and details of his book)

Will cover the context (events in Jewish and world history around the prophets life)

Will strongly cover in depth the lives of (Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel)

## Class grades will be made up of:

Reading the Prophetic books (Isaiah – Malachi)

Quizzes over the prophets and their books

Test (The Kingdoms & Empires & a Mid-term & a Final)

Home work assignments on Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel-

Create four maps –

The divided kingdoms & Surrounding nations

## The Assyrian Empire

## The Babylonian & Persian Empires

Overview Poster board layout of progression of 2 kingdoms and prophets

1 paper on the life Jeremiah (The man, his emotions, and God)
