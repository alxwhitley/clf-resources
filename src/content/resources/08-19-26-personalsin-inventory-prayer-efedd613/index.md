Healing the Broken Hearted: PERSONAL SIN & THE SPIRITUAL SIN CHECKLIST<br>
Personal Sin<br>
One of the ways the enemy gains access into our lives to oppress us is through personal sin — willful or ignorant disobedience to God, or engagement in occultic practices.<br>
Repentance: Closing the Doors Opened by Sin<br>
Repentance closes the doors that sin has opened in our lives and cuts off the enemy's access to that ground. As we identify, confess, and repent of personal sin, we give that ground back to the Lordship of Jesus Christ. “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (<span class="scripture-reference">1 John 1:9</span>, NKJV).


Spiritual Sin Checklist<br>
As you review the list of 72 behavioral sins below, pray that the Lord will convict you of any sins you still need to confess and repent of. Use this opportunity to identify and mark (perhaps with the date) any unrepentant sins as the<br>
Holy Spirit reveals them to you.<br>
These behavioral sins are identified in the following scriptural references: Ex. 20:1-17, <span class="scripture-reference">Mark 7:21-22</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Rom. 1:29-31, 1</span> <span class="scripture-reference">Cor. 6:9-10</span>, Gal.<br>

Ask God, by the power of the Holy Spirit, to reveal to you if there are other sins not on this list that you may have forgotten yet need to address. These sins remain in your subconscious for your entire life until you deal with them. As long as they are there, your spiritual gates are still open and Satan can continue to use them against you.

Be open to the ways in which God chooses to reveal things to you. He may reveal a picture of something that happened in the past that draws up feelings and/or thoughts that need to be addressed, or the name of someone may come to you to spark another memory. If anything is unclear, just ask the Holy Spirit, the Spirit of Truth, to enlighten your mind's understanding.

Prayer for Healing of Sinfulness


For each sin or group of sins identified, pray something similar to the following:


Lord God, I confess and acknowledge \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ as a sin in my life.

I am sorry for my sin and I repent of it.

I renounce this sin and give back the spiritual ground to my Lord Jesus.

In Jesus' name I pray. Amen.
