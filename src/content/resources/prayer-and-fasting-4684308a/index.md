## CHURCH LIFE CLASS

## PRAYER AND FASTING

PREFACE: There is a necessity of a strong prayer life in the believer as he walks with God. Prayer in it’s

most basic form is communication with God. It is a two way street. We need to make it our

aim to develop a strong prayer life.

## I. IMPORTANCE OF PRAYER

### A. Prayer is not an option. <span class="scripture-reference">Isa 5:7</span>

B. We are commanded to pray without ceasing. <span class="scripture-reference">I Thess 5:17</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 1:9</span>

This means we are never to be finished with prayer. It is to be a constant part of our lives.

### C. All men everywhere are to pray. <span class="scripture-reference">I Tim 2:8</span>

Jesus provided us with a great example. Lk 3:21; 5:15,16

Mk 1:35 “And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, He went out, and departed into a solitary place, and there prayed.”

If Jesus, the Son of God, needed to pray, how much more do we?

### II. WHAT IS PRAYER?

### Prayer is a discipline.

### It is a bending of the will of man before God to admit his need. <span class="scripture-reference">2 Chron 7:14</span>; Mt 6:9,10

B. Prayer is rendering homage to an all-wise and benevolent heavenly Father. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 108:5</span>

C. Prayer is spending time in conversation with God.

Conversation is a two-way communication. Mt 6:9,10

## III. WHAT ARE VARIOUS TYPES OF PRAYER?

A. Persevering prayer. <span class="scripture-reference">Phil 4:6</span>; Lk 11:5-13

B. Intercessory prayer. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 8:26-27</span>

C. In the Spirit. <span class="scripture-reference">I Cor 14:14</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 6:18</span>; Jude 20

D. Supplication. <span class="scripture-reference">I Tim 2:1, 2</span>

E. Giving thanks. <span class="scripture-reference">I Tim 2:1, 2</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Phil 4:6</span>

## IV. WHAT SHOULD BE OUR ATTITUDE IN PRAYER?

A. We should pray in faith. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 11:6</span>

B. We should pray without wavering. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 1:6-8</span>; Mk 11:24

C. We should pray according to God’s will. I Jn 5:14-16.

God’s word is His will and His will is His word.

D. We should come to the Father through Jesus Christ, our intercessor. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 7:25</span>

## V. HINDRANCES TO PRAYER

A. Asking amiss (Out of the will of God). <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 4:1-4</span>.

B. Wrong motives. Mt 6:5-8

C. Pride. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 138:6</span>

D. Iniquity. 1sa 59:1-3

E. An unforgiving Spirit. Mt 5:23, 24; 18:21-35

F. Bitterness. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 12:15-17</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">2 Pet 2:21-23</span>

G. Heart idols (covetousness). <span class="scripture-reference">Ezek 14:3</span>

H. Stinginess. <span class="scripture-reference">Prov 21:13</span>

I. Doubts. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 1:5-7</span>

J. Wrong relationships. <span class="scripture-reference">I Pet 3:7</span>

## VI. FASTING

Fasting goes hand in hand with prayer. True fasting intensifies prayer.

Mt 17:21 “Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.”

A. Proper motivation for fasting.

Isaiah 58 is called “the fasting chapter”. It gives us a description of the proper fast before God, and

the proper motivation for a fast.

### B. When should we fast?

1\. When directed by the Spirit of God. Lk 4:1-2

2\. When we know as individuals that we need to. Mt 6:16-18

3\. When the Church as a whole is called to fast for special occasions. <span class="scripture-reference">Joel 1:14</span>; 2:15,16; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 13:1-4</span>

C. Some results of fasting in Bible times.

1\. A nation was delivered from death. Esther 4

2\. The city of Ninevah was saved from divine judgment. Jonah 2-3

3\. Demon spirits were cast out. Mt 17:14-21

4\. Elders were ordained. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 14:23</span>

5\. The will of the Lord was revealed. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 9:9-15</span>.

“The Spirit said...,” as they ministered to the Lord and fasted. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 13:1-4</span>
