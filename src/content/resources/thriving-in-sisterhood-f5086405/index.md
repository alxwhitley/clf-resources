## THRIVING IN SISTERHOOD

### Barriers to Relationships:

Envy / <span class="scripture-reference">Job 5:2</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Prov 14:30</span> | resentment towards the blessings and achievements of others

Jealously / <span class="scripture-reference">I Cor 3:3</span> | not wanting to share something or someone you possess or hope to possess

Insecurities | a feeling of inadequacy (not being good enough) and uncertainty; lack of confidence

Comparison / <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 6:4-10</span> ERV | to examine the character or qualities of especially to discover resemblances or differences

Manipulate / <span class="scripture-reference">Gen 30:3</span> | negotiate, control, or influence for one’s own advantage

Offense – “trap” / <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:13</span> NLT; <span class="scripture-reference">Prov 19:11</span> | irritations and annoyances that have the inclination to build into bitterness and grudges

Gossip (Slander, Backbiting) / <span class="scripture-reference">Prov 20:19</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Prov 25:23</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 101:5</span> | Impure motive of bearing bad news behind someone’s back

Isolate myself

Constantly suspicious of people

Focus on what others have instead of being thankful for what I have

## Demanding & Controlling

Follow people on social media just to be offended by their posts?

Jealous when others are acknowledged, celebrated, or promoted (David & Saul)

How do I respond when someone forgets my birthday or anniversary?

Rehash all day when someone mistreats me?

When someone shares an achievement, do I tell of something better that I’ve done?

Make conversations all about me? Do I look forward to sharing bad things about others?
