“Then we will no longer be little children, tossed by the waves and blown around by every wind of teaching (doctrine), by human cunning and cleverness in the techniques of deceit. But speaking the truth in love, let us grow in every way into him who is the head – Christ.” <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:14-15</span> (CSB)

## Doctrine Vs Theology

Doctrine – Teaching or Instruction

Biblical Doctrine – All that the bible teaches or instructs on a particular subject<br>
Theology – The study of God, religious doctrine or matters of divinity. One’s theology can be a collection of doctrine.

What is the basic purpose of all doctrinal study?

“Many have undertaken to compile a narrative about the about the events that have been fulfilled among us…so that you may know the certainty of the things about which you have been instructed.” <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 1:1,4</span> (CSB)

To equip the believer to do good works. II Timothy 3: 16, 17

To prepare the believer to war an effective warfare against Satan. Luke 4, <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 6:17</span>

To ground our faith firmly on the Word of Truth. <span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 2:7</span>

To enable the believer to properly express love to the Lord and His people. <span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 1:5</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">I Peter 1:22</span>

To instruct the believer as to how to property function in the House of the Lord. <span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 3:14,15</span>

Why is it important for every believer to have a good biblical foundation?

“Pay close attention to your life and your teaching; persevere in these things, for in doing this you will save both yourself and your hearers” <span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 4:16</span> (CSB)

Because the last days will be characterized by deception. This is very apparent in our day with the rise of various cults and the infiltration of secular, humanistic philosophies. Matthew 24: 4,5

Because every believer is to teach and instruct others to observe the commandments of the Lord. <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 28:19, 20</span>

Because all believers should be able to give an answer when questioned concerning the hope that they have in Christ. <span class="scripture-reference">I Peter 3:15</span>

Because what we believe will affect our destiny. <span class="scripture-reference">John 14:6</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">II Peter 1:8-11</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">II Timothy 3:15</span>

What are the requirements for doctrine?

Doctrine must be sound. <span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 1:10</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">II Timothy 4:3, 4</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Titus 2:1</span>

Doctrine must be pure. <span class="scripture-reference">Titus 2:7-8</span>

Doctrine must be based on scripture. <span class="scripture-reference">II Timothy 3:14-17</span>

Doctrine must be mixed with faith. <span class="scripture-reference">I Thessalonians 2:13</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 4:2</span>

Doctrine must be practiced. <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 6:17</span> , <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:28</span>
