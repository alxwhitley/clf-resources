Healing the Broken Hearted: Prayer for Healing of Parental Wounds

## Father Wounds

The job of a father is to provide, protect, and give identity. It is necessary to pray, forgive, and release our fathers for every way in which they fell short, and we are going to allow God to father us.

~ Prayer to Heal from Father Wounds ~

Father God, thank you for being my Father.

Father, I thank you for providing for me, for protecting me, and for giving me an identity.

In this moment, I release to you my natural father in every area that he did not provide, did not protect, or did not give me an identity.

I forgive him and I release him. He owes me nothing.

Moving forward, I see You as my Father.

I will not hold my earthly father hostage anymore. I release him fully and completely.

I do not hold him responsible for who I get to become and who You are making me into.

I allow You to father me as I need to be.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

Mother Wounds<br>
The job of a mother is to comfort, nurture, and teach. It is necessary to pray, forgive, and release our mothers for every way in which they fell short, and we are going to allow God to mother us.

~ Prayer to Heal from Mother Wounds ~

Father God, thank you for being my mother.

Father, I thank you for sending Holy Spirit to comfort, nurture, and teach me.

In this moment, I release to you my natural mother in every area that she did not comfort, nurture, or teach me.

I forgive her and I release her. She owes me nothing.

Moving forward, I see You as my Mother.

I will not hold my earthly mother hostage anymore. I release her fully and completely.

I do not hold her responsible for who I get to become, and who You are making me into.

I allow You to mother me as I need to be.

In Jesus' name, Amen.
