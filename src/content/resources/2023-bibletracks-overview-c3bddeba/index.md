Bible T.R.A.C.K.S. Series 2023<br>
SUNDAYS: 9AM

Quarter 1

April 16 What is the Gospel? Scott Woodard<br>
A foundational teaching that defines and clarifies the Gospel as the central message of the Bible;<br>
reviews enduring truths of Creation, the Fall, and Redemption as critical underpinnings to the proclamation (good news) of Jesus’ life, death, resurrection, and present-day ministry. Refutes errors and heresies relevant to 21st century culturalism.

April 23 Proclaiming the Gospel, Sharing Your Faith Timothy Majors

Explores practical ways and means of witnessing to unbelievers; highlights the value of sharing one’s faith in Jeus Christ and clearly communicating the Gospel; helps believers to identify and articulate critical components of their personal testimony.

## April 30 Defending the Faith Joseph Hancock

Introduces believers to apologetics; reviews major proofs of Christianity and important defenses related to the existence & involvement of God in creation, the inerrancy & inspiration of Scripture, historical evidence of the life, death, & resurrection of Jesus Christ, and the overarching necessity of faith.

Quarter 2

September 10, 17, 24 Breaking Free Series Kim Kidd / Nelson Masinde

Uncovers keys to personal freedom, emotional healing, and spiritual breakthrough, specifically by identifying areas of offence (hurts/wounds) and responding with forgiveness (9/10); identifying and addressing the ungodly thoughts and behavioral patterns of spiritual strongholds (9/17); and identifying and breaking inherited bondages/generational curses (9/24).

Quarter 3

November 12 The Sermon on the Mount Shabaka Williams

Outlines principles of kingdom living that Jesus taught in His first public sermon: covers attitudes and spiritual dispositions of a believer; highlights the identity and functions of a disciple in the world; and reviews godly responses in various dynamics of relationship.

CLF Bible T.R.A.C.K.S is a ministry of teaching and instruction designed for Believers interested in deepening their understanding and application of biblical truth.
