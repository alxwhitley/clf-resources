## Review – Law- History Books – Poetry Books

Kids will dress up like a Character that is assigned to them and tell the story of the bible to the class.

The groups will work together to create an outline of the Old Testament and the pick among themselves to be a character and tell their part of the story. – Use Study notes to review info about the characters so you know what information to say.

Sarah - (Tell of Abraham, lot, Isaac, Ishmael, Abram’s covenant with God, and story of Job)

Jacob – (Tell of Esau, Rachel, twelve children)

Joseph – (Tell of brothers, time in Egypt, Jacob coming to Egypt)

Miriam – (Tell the story of Moses, Deliverance of Egypt, Crossing Red Sea)

Joseph – (Tell wilderness Experience- Spying new land, Possessing Canaan)

Deborah – (Tell of the days of the judges)

David- (Tell of Saul, Samuel, the days of the Kings, Psalms)

Solomon –(The building of the temple, rise and fall of Israel, The splitting of the nation, Eccl + Proverbs + Song of Solomon)

Esther- (Tell of the days of Ezra, Nehemiah and her own story)
