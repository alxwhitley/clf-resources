Godhead and Progressive Revelation

Definition- Refers to that which is divine, to deity, involving God’s revelation of His own mode of being (<span class="scripture-reference">Col 2:9</span>/<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 1:19-20</span>)

Anything that can be known about God comes from him revealing it to us. The Word of God has revealed what can be known about God and his nature to us.

The nature of God has been progressively revealed throughout the Bible. The nature of God’s essence has evolved over time up to the incarnation of Jesus Christ (<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 1:3</span>/<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 1:19</span>)

God has revealed himself as one God, existing in three persons: The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirt; distinguishable but indivisible in essence; co-eternal, co-existent, co-equal in nature, attributes, power, and glory.
