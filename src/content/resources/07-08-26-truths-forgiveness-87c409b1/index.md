Healing the Broken Hearted: Truths on Forgiveness

Forgiveness is not optional. As a child of God you NEED to forgive others:

1) It is in the nature and redemptive plan of God to forgive humanity; as children, we must reflect His nature.
2) As an act of faith and in accordance with the principals of sowing and reaping, God’s children are to continually practice forgiving others
3) Forgiveness is the willful act of releasing those who have sinned against us; in forgiving others, we release the individual from owing us anything and free them from being obligated to us in accordance with their sin.
4) Forgiveness DOES NOT:
a. Justify or excuse sinful behavior.<br>
b. Result in or require automatic reconciliation of relationships.<br>
c. Dismiss the need for maintaining clear, healthy boundaries as necessary.
