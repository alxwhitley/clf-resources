## CHURCH LIFE CLASS

## PRAISE AND WORSHIP

PREFACE: Praise and worship hold a very important place among God’s people. The Bible gives us a

thorough coverage of praise and worship. There has been a great hunger among God’s

people to return to a more Biblical expression of praise and worship. We believe that God

desires us to worship in a Psalmic pattern, which is a genuine, heart felt, expressive praise and

worship desired by God and described in detail in the book of Psalms. Let us look at part of

the Biblical pictures of praise and worship and pattern ourselves after them.

## I. INTRODUCTION TO PRAISE AND WORSHIP

## A. Importance of praise and worship.

Satan knew the value of worship. He offered Jesus all the kingdoms of the world and their glory if

he would fall down and worship him. Mt 4:8-11

The importance of praise and worship lies in the relationship it establishes between the worshiper and the object worshiped. We tend to become like what we worship. Example of a fan and an athletic team.

B. Real worship and praise are the characteristics of true Christianity.

<span class="scripture-reference">Phil 3:3</span> “for we are the true circumcision; who worship in the Spirit of God and glory in Christ Jesus and put no confidence in the flesh.”

Jesus in talking to woman at the well explains that the time has now come that true worshipers will not be set apart by outward form. True worshipers will worship God in Spirit and Truth.

Jn 4:21-24.

C. Distinctions between worship and praise.

## 1\. Definitions:

Praise - to commend, to applaud, to express approval or admiration of, to extol in

words or in song, to magnify, shine, boast, rave etc. It is an outward expression.

Worship - The Greek word carries the meaning to kiss, like a dog licking his master’s hand. Also,

it means to do reverence to, adore, fawn or crouch, worship etc. It is difficult to get a

simple definition of worship because real worship defies definition. It is something to be

experienced. It is the result of a divine encounter that involves the feelings that are born

out an intimate communion with God. It is a response to a revelation of God.

Praise and worship are so closely related and affect each other so much that it is difficult to

completely separate them into narrow boxes of definition.

D. How are we to worship God?

Jesus tells us how to worship in <span class="scripture-reference">John 4:24</span>.

1\. Worship in spirit - Jesus here is showing that worship is not to be bound to a certain time or place,

but it was going to be a function of the spirit of man reaching out to the Spirit of God.

Worship isn’t just outward ritual. Worship is our spirit corresponding with God’s Spirit.

Worship in Truth - Jesus Christ is the truth. Jn 14:6. Jesus intends that we worship in

accordance with the truths of God’s word. Jn 17:17, Ps 119. The doctrinal content of our

songs determine their real spiritual value.

3\. Worship with our whole heart. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 111:1</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 9:1</span> It includes our emotions.

E. Praise and worship signify our priestly ministry.

1\. The Bible teaches that we are all priests unto God. <span class="scripture-reference">Rev 1:6</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">I Pet 2:5</span>

2\. God has shown that He is to be approached by sacrifice and spiritual sacrifices. <span class="scripture-reference">1 Pet 2:5</span>

\- Sacrifice ourselves. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 12:1</span>

\- Sacrifice our possessions. <span class="scripture-reference">Phil 4:18</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 13:16</span>

\- Sacrifice of praise. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 13:15</span>

\- Sacrifice of thanksgiving. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 116:1</span> 7; <span class="scripture-reference">I Thess 5:18</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 5:20</span>

\- Sacrifice of joy. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 27:6</span>

## II. WHY WE ARE TO WORSHIP

1\. God has ordained worship/praise as a way to come into His presence. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 100:4</span>

2\. Worship/praise is a sign that God is in the midst of us (<span class="scripture-reference">Ps 22:3</span>). God inhabits our praise

(<span class="scripture-reference">Jer 33:11</span>).

3\. God has given us worship as a way of glorifying Him. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 50:23</span>

4\. Worship/praise is part of our priestly ministry. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 13:15</span>

5\. God is worthy to be praised. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 18:3</span>

## III. WHEN AND WHERE WE ARE TO WORSHIP

### A. When are we to worship?

1\. At all times. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 34:1</span>

2\. Continually. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 34:1</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 35:7</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 13:15</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 5:18,20</span>; Lk 24:53

3\. While I have my being. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 146:1-3</span>; 45:17; 71:6; 119:164

4\. Everyday. “Everyday will I bless Thee” <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 146:1-3</span>

B. Where we are to worship?

1\. In our homes. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 149:5</span>

2\. In the congregation. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 22:22</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 35:18</span>; 100:2,4; 111:1; 134:2; 135:1

3\. Before the unbeliever. <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 2:46-47</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 40:3</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 126:2</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Acts 16:25-34</span>

C. Who is to worship?

1\. The Lord’s servants. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 113:1</span>

2\. Those who fear the Lord. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 22:23</span>

3\. Those who seek Him. <span class="scripture-reference">Ps 22:26</span>; 145:10

## IV. HOW WE ARE TO WORSHIP

Controversy stirs when we begin to discuss the manner in which we are to worship God especially the

outward expressions. We believe in “Biblical Psalmic Worship” which means a genuine, heart felt,

expressive praise and worship desired by God and described in the Bible, especially the book of Psalms.

This is part of worshipping Him according to the word.
