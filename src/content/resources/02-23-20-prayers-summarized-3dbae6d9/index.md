Prayer of Purity<br>
Father wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. Purge me by the Blood of Jesus, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. Hide your face from my sins and blot out all my iniquities. Lord, create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me. Cast me not away from Your presence; and take not Your Holy Spirit from me. Restore unto me the joy of Your salvation; and uphold me with Your free spirit. (<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 51:2, 7-12</span>)<br>
Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts. And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting. (<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 139:23-24</span>)<br>
Teach me Your way, O LORD; I will walk in Your truth: unite my heart to fear Your name. (Psalm<br>
86:11)<br>
Prayer of Blessing<br>
Oh that You would bless me indeed, and enlarge my coast, and that Your hand might be with me, and that You would keep me from evil, that it may not grieve me. (<span class="scripture-reference">1 Chronicles. 4:10</span>)<br>
The Lord bless you, and keep you: The Lord make his face shine upon you, and be gracious unto you: The Lord lift up his countenance upon you, and give you peace. (<span class="scripture-reference">Numbers 6:24-26</span>)<br>
Prayers for Growth in the House of God<br>
Father for those who have committed sin in our midst and before You, we ask for life—for the new life afforded by salvation, for life that comes by the Spirit and cancels out the wages of sin, and for repentance that produces transformation and eternal life in the end. (<span class="scripture-reference">1 John 5:16</span>)<br>
Father we pray that our faith would not fail, that you would bring about true conversions in Your disciples and empower us to strengthen our brothers and sisters in this Body. (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 22:32</span>)<br>
Father according to the riches of Your glory strengthen us with might by Your Spirit in the inner man; cause Christ to dwell in our hearts by faith; that we might be rooted and grounded in love, and able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height of<br>
Your love; cause us to know and experience the love of Christ, which passes all head knowledge— and fill us with all the fulness of God. (<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 3:16-19</span>)<br>
Father we pray that our love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all judgment;<br>
enable us to approve things that are excellent; cause us to be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ; Father fill us with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ, for Your the glory and praise O God. (<span class="scripture-reference">Philippians 1:9-11</span>)<br>
Father fill us with the knowledge of Your will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding, so that we might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God; Lord, strengthen us with all might, according to Your glorious power, unto all patience and longsuffering with joyfulness. Father helps us to continuously give you thanks as we realize how You have enabled us to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light. (<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 1:9-12</span>)


Prayer of Corporate Dedication<br>
Lord God we ask that You will be with us just as You were our fathers of the faith: please do not leave us, nor forsake us: Lord God incline our hearts to You, to walk in all Your ways, and to keep<br>
Your commandments, statutes, judgments. Let our covenant commitment keep us close to You<br>
Lord God day and night—Lord maintain the cause of Your servants, and the cause of Your covenant people at all times and as the matter require; if and when we fail or stumble, lead us in repentance, forgive us, and in mercy empower us to stand in Your faithfulness. (<span class="scripture-reference">1 Kings 8:57-60</span>)<br>
Prayer for the Fate of the Nation<br>
Lord, it is nothing for You to help, whether with many or with few, or weather it’s with the powerless against the might. We acknowledge our powerlessness in America and ask You to help us, O Lord our God. Help in every sphere of our society—spiritually, politically, economically, socially, and culturally—we cry out for help! God in heaven we trust in You and in the Name of<br>
Jesus name we stand against the spiritual enemy that fights against this nation. O Lord You are our<br>
God; let Your will be done and do not man prevail against You. (<span class="scripture-reference">2 Chronicles 14:11</span>)<br>
Prayer for Governmental Leaders<br>
Father we thank you for all the governing authorities in our land—from elected and unelected federal officials, to state government leaders, and local officials and leaders. Thank you Lord for their service and sacrifice. We pray on their behalf that their service would be directed by You and characterized by righteousness and integrity—cause them to lead, serve, and legislate in such a way that the citizens of our region, state, and country are able to live peaceable lives in all godliness and honesty. Anoint, equip, and guide American leaders so that which is good and acceptable in sight of God happens in our country and throughout the world today; may the gospel of Jesus Christ Our Savior prevail and be proclaimed so that many people will be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth. (<span class="scripture-reference">1 Timothy 2:1-4</span>)<br>
Prayer for the Indwelling of the Holy Spirit<br>
Father, please give us the gift of the Holy Spirit. We ask and invite Your Spirit to fill us continually, lead us daily, and to pray through us often. We ask that our walk, our worship, and our ways would be directed and empowered by the Holy Spirit. Holy Spirit cover us, flow through us, teach us, speak through us, convict us, comfort us, seal us, reveal to us the mind of God, and keep us in the love of God in Jesus’ Name. (<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:9-13</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 8:26-28</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Jude 1:20-21</span>)<br>
Prayer for the Presence of God<br>
Father as we walk forward in obedience to Your will, living in relationship and community with the people You have called us to, we pray that we will know you intimately and be known by You personally and corporately. Grant us an awareness of Your Presence, of You Spirit with us. Be<br>
Emmanuel (God with Us) to the people of in this church—characterize our lives with Your presence. Also give us physical, tangible manifestations of Your Presence that transforms our hearts, strengthens our faith, revitalizes our hope, restores our joy, and provides the rest that<br>
You’ve promised. (<span class="scripture-reference">Exodus 33:12-17</span>)
