## SUBMISSION IN GODLY RELATIONSHIPS

A. Walking together with other believers in submitted relationships is essential to the process of growth and maturity.

Relationships are the "school of the Spirit" through which God not only brings needed nourishment but also exposes weaknesses to be dealt with which produces growth and maturity.

B. In God's scheme of things He has made us part of one another.

<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 12:5</span> 5 so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and individually members one of another. (NASB) Amplified "..mutually dependent on one another.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:25</span> 25 Therefore, laying aside falsehood, speak the truth, each one of you, with his neighbor, for we are members of one another. (NASB)

There is a God-designed need for the input of others in our lives.

C. There must be submission and humility in our relationships if we are to receive nourishment from others in our lives.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 5:21</span> - and be subject to one another in the fear of Christ. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Peter 5:5</span> 5 Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble. (KJV)

D. Three important ideas about walking together in humble/submitted relationships.

1\. God has designed us in such a way that the influence and input of others in our lives is critical and used by God to help us grow.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:16</span> 16 from whom the whole body, being fitted and held together by that which every joint supplies, according to the proper working of each individual part, causes the growth of the body for the building up of itself in love. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 2:19</span> 19 and not holding fast to the head, from whom the entire body, being supplied and held together by the joints and ligaments, grows with a growth which is from God. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 3:16</span> 16 Let the word of Christ richly dwell within you, with all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with thankfulness in your hearts to God. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 3:12-13</span> 12 Take care, brethren, lest there should be in any one of you an evil, unbelieving heart, in falling away from the living God. 13 But encourage one another day after day, as long as it is still called "Today," lest any one of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 10:24</span> 24 and let us consider how to stimulate one another to love and good deeds, (NASB)

2\. We benefit from others perspective and experience in walking with God.

<span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 3:17-18</span> 17 so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; and that you, being rooted and grounded in love, 18 may be able to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth, (NASB)

In any investigation, you want to try to get as many witnesses in order to get as complete a picture as possible of what happened and what the suspect looked like.

3\. God uses the influence of others to correct, admonish, encourage, and challenge us if we are failing to hit the mark.

In <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 2:11</span>, Paul rebukes Peter for something he was doing that missed the mark. If Peter one of the three closest friends of the Lord, needed a rebuke, it is also possible that we may at some point in our walk.

E. In the Church there is also the authority and protection of God given leadership.

<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 13:17</span> 17 Obey your leaders, and submit to them; for they keep watch over your souls, as those who will give an account. Let them do this with joy and not with grief, for this would be unprofitable for you. (NASB)

<span class="scripture-reference">1 Thessalonians 5:12</span> 12 But we request of you, brethren, that you appreciate those who diligently labor among you, and have charge over you in the Lord and give you instruction, (NASB)

F. Beware of an independent mindset.

This was the problem in <span class="scripture-reference">Col 2:16-23</span>.

These people are not following God's plan for His Church (vs 23). They have an appearance of wisdom and elements of truth but are lacking the full picture of God's plan for His Church.

Independent people are not connected and are not receiving the vital input of others to help them grow. This puts them in a dangerous position.

People of an independent mindset often display an emphasis on "My Ministry" without realizing that ministry exists to further the needs of the Church.

Things that cause an independent mindset - lack of vision, pride, rebellion, covering of sin, etc.

G. The New Testament gives us very clear guidelines on how we are to walk in relationships together as a Local Church.

1\. Be devoted to and prefer one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 2:10</span>

2\. Be of the same mind and live in harmony. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 12:16</span>

3\. Love one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 13:8</span>

4\. Don't judge one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 14:13</span>

5\. Pursue harmony and build up one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 14:19</span>

6\. Be like-minded and sympathetic to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:5</span>

7\. Accept one another, like Christ accepted you. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:7</span>

8\. Admonish, council or instruct one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Rom 15:14</span>

9\. Care for one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Cor 12:25</span>

10\. Serve one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Gal 5:13</span>

11\. Bear each other's burdens. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 6:2</span>

12\. Bear with each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:2</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:12</span>

13\. Be kind and helpful to one another, and forgive each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 4:32</span>

14\. Be accountable to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Eph 5:21</span>

15\. Be honest, don't lie to one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:9</span>

16\. Teach and admonish one another. <span class="scripture-reference">Col 3:16</span>

17\. Increase in love for one another. 1Thes 3:12

18\. Comfort and cheer up one another. 1Thes 4:18

19\. Exhort and encourage each other daily. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 3:13</span>

20\. Stimulate and provoke others to love and good works. <span class="scripture-reference">Heb 19:24</span>

21\. Don't speak evil about each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 4:11</span>

22\. Don't complain about each other. <span class="scripture-reference">Jas 5:9</span>

23\. Love one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 1:22</span>; 1Jn 3:11; 3:23; 4:7; 4:11; 4:12; 2Jn 5

24\. Be hospitable towards one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 4:9</span>

25\. Be submitted to and humble towards one another. <span class="scripture-reference">1Pet 5:5</span>

It is obvious that in order to walk in these instructions for fellowship it will take more than just a corporate meeting one or two times a week. It is to be a way of life.
