GOSPEL OF JOHN – STUDY ACTIVITIES
1) Treasury of Knowledge Study – Mondays
 Select a verse or passage from the daily readings and utilize the TSK feature in<br>
Blue Letter Bible App.<br>
 Read all of the cross-referenced verses.<br>
 Select 1 or more of the cross-referenced verses and delve deeper by reading the associated passage or entire chapter for enhanced understanding and context.
2) Meditation Exercise – Tuesdays/Thursdays/Sundays
 <span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 19:14</span>, Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, my strength, and my redeemer.<br>
 Pray – ask the Holy Spirit to assist you in knowing/understanding His Word<br>
 Read – read aloud a verse or short passage from the daily readings.<br>
 Listen – identify any meaningful word, phrase, or intent that the Holy Spirit impresses upon your mind or speaks to you.<br>
 Pray – talk to God specifically, pray out of the context of the verse or passage<br>
 Reflect/Rest – Rest in God’s presence & the truth of His Word at work in you
3) Chat Your Insights - Wednesdays
 Using the chat feature in the Word Explosion Group, share pertinent insights or understanding that you have gleaned from any of the readings or study activities each week.<br>
 Read other’s shared insights; feel free to reply.
4) Commentary Study – Fridays
 Commentaries are extended discussions presented by scholars and theologians.<br>
 Select a verse or passage from any of the daily readings and utilize the “Text<br>
Commentary” or “Audio / Video” feature to access associated commentary material.
5) Verse of the Day or Asks Questions – Any day
 Use the chat string for Questions to post any pertinent questions in the Word<br>
Explosion group.
