## Old Testament Survey - Overview of the Pentateuch

### The Pentateuch (Greek) means - 5 Rolls = Law of Moses

The Torah (Hebrew) means - Law = Law of Moses

## The Pentateuch consist of 5 books:

Genesis - The book of the Origin of Man / Patriarchs / Beginning of Israel

Exodus - The way out of Bondage

Leviticus - Worship under a Levitical order

Numbers - the 2 census & Organization of Israel’s wilderness wonderings

Deuteronomy - 2nd Giving of the Law

### Genesis - Creation – Death of Joseph, 4004 BC – 1689 BC, 2315yrs

### Exodus - From Joseph’s death –Tabernacle, 1706 –1490 BC, 216 yrs

### Leviticus - Less than 1 year at Mt Sinai – 1490BC

### Numbers - Mt Sinai – Plains of Moab, 1490 – 1451 BC, 39 yrs wilderness

### Deuteronomy - 2 months at the Plains of Moab, 1451 BC

JEDP Theory – Multiple manuscripts

(In spite of the bible & Jesus himself giving credit to Moses as the author of the Pentateuch) some critics still suggest that the Pentateuch was not written by one author ( Moses) but is a composite of 4 different manuscripts edited together.

JEDP Theory – says that different authors wrote 4 different manuscripts over 500 years. From the 9-5 century. Then where edited together about 400 BC into one manuscript.

J – manuscript, Alleged to have been written about 850 BC. It was named for the author’s use of the divine word of Yahweh or Jehovah.

E - Manuscript, Alleged to have been written about 750 BC, 100 years later. Named for the author’s use of referring to God as Elohim.

D – Manuscript, Consist of the major part of Deuteronomy. Believed to have been written about 621 BC (130 years later)

P – Manuscript, Believed to be the work of a priest. This manuscript makes up the majority of the Pentateuch. Believed to have been written about 500-450 BC.

The above assumptions are strictly based on the dictation and style of the manuscripts. The major arguments are based that the different portions of text often refer to God in different names and reflections of personalities.

Example: Gen. 1 & 2 are supposed to have been written by different authors because of the context of the way God is referred to.

Genesis 1: refers to God as Elohim and Genesis 2: refers to God as Yahweh. So Genesis 1 is referred to as a P- Manuscript and Genesis 2 Is referred to as a J- Manuscript

The Scriptures and the quotes of Jesus himself in the New Testament referring to the Pentateuch as the writings of Moses – offers enough authenticity and credibility to Moses and his writings. Moses is the accepted author of the whole Law.

Moses would have easily had the past documents of the Hebrew history from the days of Jacob when he came to Egypt. (Gen 46:). He could have pulled the information for the genealogies to write part of the Book of Genesis from the information from the 10 Generation of Genesis.
