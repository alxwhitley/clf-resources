C. The Position of Prayer

Our position, purpose, or function in prayer may vary as the Holy Spirits leads or as the situation may merit. Recognizing and engaging the right position is necessary for our spiritual growth, maturity, and contribution to the Body of Christ. The position that we take in prayer can reveal our expectation, motivation, and alignment with the will of God.

i. Personal/Devotional Prayer – prayer “for you”, not necessarily about you or your needs, but prayer that ministers to you and strengths you.<br>
a. <span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 55:17</span>, Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud:<br>
and he shall hear my voice.<br>
b. <span class="scripture-reference">Jude 1:20-21</span>, But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost, Keep yourselves in the love of God, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.<br>
c. <span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 35:13</span>, But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth:<br>
I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into mine own bosom.<br>
ii. Petitions – Prayer requests, asking in a relationship of trust and dependence a. <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 6:18</span>, Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;<br>
b. <span class="scripture-reference">Philippians 4:6</span>, Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto<br>
God.<br>
c. <span class="scripture-reference">1 Timothy 2:1</span>, I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men;<br>
