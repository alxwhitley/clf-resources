## WHAT IS A “TITHE”?

“Tithe” comes from a Hebrew word which means tenth. A tithe of one’s income (or increase); therefore, is one-tenth (10%). Tithing refers to the practice of systematically giving 10% of one’s income back to the Lord for His work.

Tithing was the common practice of holy men of God in Old Testament days.

Prior to the giving of the law, God’s people tithed voluntarily.

Abraham gave a tenth to Melchizedek (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 14:18-20</span>)

Jacob gave a tenth at Bethel (<span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 14:18-20</span>)

When God gave the law to Moses, tithing was a major part of His overall financial system (<span class="scripture-reference">Leviticus 27:30-33</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Numbers 18:20-32</span>)

Jesus confirmed tithing in His teaching and practice (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 23:23</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:42</span>; 18:12; <span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 7:1-21</span>)

<span class="scripture-reference">Luke 11:42</span> “You Pharisees are hypocrites! For you are obsessed with peripheral issues, like paying meticulous tithes on the smallest herbs that grow in your gardens.\[a\] Of course, these matters you should do, but when you unjustly cheat others, you ignore the most important duty of all: to walk in the love of God.\[b\] Readjust your values and place first things first.

Tithings is to serve as a reminder that all we have comes from and belongs to the Lord. (<span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 8:11, 17-18</span>)

Tithing and giving is God’s cure for covetousness. (<span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 6:19-21</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Ephesians 4:28</span>)

Tithings is the beginning of our giving (<span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 12:6</span>). Freewill offerings and giving to the poor should also be part of our giving (<span class="scripture-reference">Galatians 2:10</span>)

## WARNINGS & MISCONCEPTIONS CONCERNING MONEY

The Bible clearly teaches us that money is not evil, but the love of money more than our love for God will cause us to stumble in the area of covetousness. (Covet – to desire something and want to take it for yourself).

We are warned not to make the accumulation of wealth our main ambition in life. (Luke

12:16-21; <span class="scripture-reference">1 Timothy 6:9</span>).

We are warned not to value material gain above spiritual gain (<span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 6:6, 11</span>)

We are warned that a love for money will end in destruction (<span class="scripture-reference">I Timothy 6:9-11</span>)

Some say you can give money wherever you want but God says there’s a designated place (<span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 12:5, 6, 13, 14</span>)

Some say that you can designate on your envelope where the tithe is to be used, but God says the tithe is to go for the service of His house (<span class="scripture-reference">Numbers 18:21-24</span>).

Some say you can borrow your tithes and pay them back later, but God says if you do borrow them, when you pay them back, you must add a fifth more to the initial tithe (<span class="scripture-reference">Leviticus 27:31</span>).

Some say that God doesn’t care what we give, only how we give, but God says that He is concerned with both (<span class="scripture-reference">Mark 12:41-44</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 5:20</span>).

WHAT ARE SOME CLEAR NEW TESTAMENT PRINCIPLES THAT SHOULD GOVERN OUR GIVING?

The New Testament, in every case elevates the Old Testament practices to a higher and more spiritual level. Rather than giving merely because we have to, there are other factors that believers need to consider. We should give:

Ourselves to the Lord first (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 8:5</span>)

Willingly (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 8:3, 12</span>)

Cheerfully (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 9:7</span>)

Generously, liberally (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 8:2</span>; 9:13)

Proportionately (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 16:1-2</span>)

Regularly (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 9:7</span>)

Systematically (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 9:7</span>)

Lovingly (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 8:24</span>)

Thankfully (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 9:11, 12</span>)

Sacrificially (<span class="scripture-reference">Hebrews 13:16</span>)

As a Ministry to the Lord and His saints (<span class="scripture-reference">II Corinthians 9:12, 13</span>).
