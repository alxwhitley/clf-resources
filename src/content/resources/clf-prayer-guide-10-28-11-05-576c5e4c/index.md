## Tuesday, 10/29 Righteous Elected Officials

(<span class="scripture-reference">1 Timothy 2:1-3</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 12:26, 14</span>:34, 16:12, 21:3; <span class="scripture-reference">2 Chronicles 36:22</span>-<br>
23; <span class="scripture-reference">Romans 13:1-7</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Esther 4:14-16</span>)

❖ Pray that righteous Christian men and women will be elected to office.<br>
❖ Pray that any unsaved elected officials will be “friends of the church” and maintain or enact policies favorable for the propagation of the gospel.<br>
❖ Pray for the salvation of all presidential candidates/running mates, judges, legislators, commissioners, and school board officials.<br>
❖ Pray that God will give grace, conviction, and courage to elected<br>
Christians as they serve and rule in a culture resistant to righteousness.



Wednesday, 10/30 Sanctity of Life

(<span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 30:19</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Leviticus 18:21</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Jeremiah 32:34-36</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Jeremiah 1:5</span>;
<span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 139:1-17</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Genesis 4:1, 25</span>:21-24)

❖ Pray that the spirit of murder and child sacrifice through abortion will be completely disarmed in our state and across the country; repent on behalf of the nation and citizens that chose to declare that such evil was good.<br>
❖ Pray that righteous legislation will be supported and enacted to protect the life of the unborn.<br>
❖ Pray that the Holy Spirit will convict men and women who are personally considering an abortion and empower them to choose life.<br>
Likewise, pray that repentance, healing, and forgiveness will come to those who have participated in abortion.<br>
❖ Pray that Choose Life Raleigh will grow through God’s provision of leadership, administration, critical resources/partnerships and ongoing vision to serve women and families who reject abortion and choose to preserve the life of their unborn child.

Thursday, 10/31 Integrity in the Election Process

(<span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 25:15</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 11:1, 3, 16</span>:11, 33, 19:5; <span class="scripture-reference">Amos 5:12-15</span>;
<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 12:17</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">2 Corinthians 13:7</span>, <span class="scripture-reference">Exodus 20:16</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Zechariah 8:16</span>)

❖ Pray that election poll workers and those who count ballots will operate with clear motives, integrity, equity, and a righteous work- ethic.<br>
❖ Pray for righteous and fair reporting with every news and media outlet.<br>
❖ Pray that corruption and deceptive practices will be exposed.<br>
❖ Pray that truth will prevail, and that voters will not be deceived by mudslinging, rumors, and/or lies.<br>
❖ Pray that constituents will vote honestly; pray that Christians will vote with Spirit-led, biblical convictions.



## Friday, 11/1 The United States Supreme Court

(<span class="scripture-reference">Habakkuk 1:4</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Psalms 119:136</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">John 7:24</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 1:16, 16</span>:18, 25:1; <span class="scripture-reference">Psalms 2:10, 119</span>:136; <span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 8:16</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Isaiah 1:26</span>)

❖ Pray that the Supreme Court will uphold the sanctity of marriage as a union between one man and one woman.<br>
❖ Pray for the Supreme Court Justices by name: John Roberts (Chief<br>
Justice), Clarence Thomas, Samuel Alito, Sonia Sotomayor, Elena<br>
Kagan, Neil Gorsuch, Brett Kavanaugh, Amy Coney Barrett, and<br>
Ketanji Brown Jackson<br>
❖ Pray for the health, safety, and wisdom of Supreme Court Justices.<br>
❖ Pray that God-fearing Justices will be appointed to the bench.

(<span class="scripture-reference">Romans 13:1-7</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Colossians 1:16</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Exodus 18:21-22</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 6:10</span>; 1

❖ Pray that the Father’s kingdom would come and that His will would be done at the local level, specifically in each city, municipality, and county in North Carolina.<br>
❖ Pray that elected mayors, city council persons, commissioners, and other civic leaders will serve with the fear of God, be above reproach, and do what is best for their constituents.<br>
❖ Pray for righteousness to prevail at the executive, legislative, and judicial levels of our state government.<br>
❖ Pray that local elected officials will be influenced and impacted by the church, which is “the pillar and ground of the truth”.

## Monday, 11/4 North Carolina School Systems

(<span class="scripture-reference">Proverbs 22:6</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">1 Corinthians 10:23</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Deuteronomy 6:5-8</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Matthew 5:13-16</span>;
<span class="scripture-reference">Ezra 8:21-22</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">1 Chronicles 12:32</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Daniel 2:48-49</span>; <span class="scripture-reference">Psalm 91:9-11</span>)

❖ Pray that Christian students in the public schools will stand for what is right, overcome temptations, and serve as powerful witnesses for<br>
Jesus.<br>
❖ Pray that Christian teachers will function as salt and light in the public sector, influencing students for good and modeling righteousness.<br>
❖ Pray for divine protection and safety over every school in the state;<br>
rebuke darkness and evil that leads to school gun violence and death.<br>
❖ Pray that righteousness would prevail in school boards across the state; ask God to install godly leaders like the Hebrew Boys and the<br>
Sons of Issachar.<br>
❖ Pray that non-public school students and families would experience a revival of God’s Spirit and take full advantage of transformed learning environments where the Gospel is openly proclaimed, and<br>
Christ exalted.<br>

## Tuesday, 11/5 Election Day Supplications

❖ Pray for the restoration/preservation of a God-fearing democracy in the United States of America.<br>
❖ Pray that the mercy of God will reign over the nation and that His perfect will shall manifest in the 2024 election results.<br>
❖ Pray that election officials and poll workers will operate with grace, wisdom, and integrity.<br>
❖ Pray for the safety of all voters and candidates.<br>
❖ Pray for peace to prevail in Washington DC and that rioting/anarchy and violence will not arise in any city due to discontentment.<br>
❖ Pray that Christians across the land will be stirred to stand in faith, to contend for righteousness, and to exercise their civic duty by voting.
